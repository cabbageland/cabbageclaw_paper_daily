## Ranked papers

### 1. **Permanent and Transient Representations for Continual Reinforcement Learning**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant RL/continual-learning mechanism. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve. **Most relevant today.**
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.07327](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07327), November 5, 2026; NeurIPS 2026. [Author-linked code](https://github.com/NishanthVAnand/pt_representations_crl).
- **Mechanism:** Add a slow neural action-value estimate to a fast residual stored in slot-based MinHash tables. Token overlap determines local generalization; per-slot tags and LRU eviction distinguish observation identity from approximate similarity. Periodic consolidation transfers corrections into the neural component, then decays transient values.
- **Evidence:** On Craftax-Classic, return exceeds 10 before 50M steps and reaches 15 within 150M; PQN(32) needs over 200M for 15. Large-scale curves use 10 seeds; the two-stream experiment uses three. Image-task ablations compare neural residuals, table-only learning, unslotted hashing, EWC, continual backprop, and NEC.
- **Catch:** At 1024 streams, the implementation is about twice as slow as PQN in wall-clock time. Tokenization is especially natural for symbolic grid observations; the image experiment quantizes grayscale pixels. The convergence result covers linear prediction under known task boundaries and mixing/two-timescale assumptions—not the deep controller with hidden boundaries. The author-linked GitHub repository is reachable, but no code reproduction was performed.
- **Read scope:** Main text, theorem assumptions and proof outline, transient-memory algorithms, image-task architecture, Craftax tuning and wall-clock appendix. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/permanent_transient_representations_continual_rl.md).

### 2. **Expanding the Quality–Coverage Frontier with Competitive Flow Matching**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant generative modeling. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.07055](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07055), November 4, 2026. [Project](https://lilydaytoy.github.io/CFM/).
- **Mechanism:** At a fixed noisy state, draw eight low-dimensional auxiliary codes and regress only the velocity prediction closest to the target. At inference, hold one code fixed along the trajectory; its conditioning scale supplies a coverage knob that can be combined with conventional guidance. This is competing predictions in one network, not eight deployed generators.
- **Evidence:** On ImageNet-256 with 50K evaluation samples, SiT-based FD-r,6 falls from 19.22 to 5.91 near 67% recall; on RAEv2, from 16.83 at 69.09% recall to 3.18 at 70.32%. RAEv2's best overall FD-r,6 improves only from 2.07 to 2.02: the important gain is the broad-coverage operating point, not a universal quality leap.
- **Catch:** The large gains combine post-training, auxiliary-scale selection, and guidance. K=8 costs about 3.674 times ordinary FM model-core compute per update; the approximately 2.63% pretraining-compute figure compares against a very long original training run. ImageNet coverage metrics do not establish semantic compositionality or rare-event completeness. Exact conditional-mean velocity is valid FM theory; this is a finite-model intervention, not a disproof of flow matching.
- **Read scope:** Main text, auxiliary parameterization, evaluation protocol, code-consistency discussion, and compute appendix. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/competitive_flow_matching_quality_coverage.md).

### 3. **Backbone-Informed Adaptation after EEG Pretraining: A Capacity-Controlled Study**

- **Classification:** Direct neuro-AI evaluation relevance; adjacent experimental-design inspiration. **Verdict:** Useful lighter recommendation; no detailed note today.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.07192](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07192), November 4, 2026. [Author-linked code](https://github.com/0oJimmyo0/EEGxPlore-TMLR).
- **Mechanism:** Freeze CBraMod or LaBraM and compare channel/temporal-axis attention adapters against token-wise residual MLPs with nearly identical parameter counts. Hold classifier, splits, optimizer recipe, and seed pairing fixed. This tests whether structural alignment adds value beyond additional trainable capacity.
- **Evidence:** Across three paired seeds per condition, CBraMod differences are small relative to run variation. For LaBraM, the native adapter loses balanced accuracy to the MLP on FACED, 0.1613 versus 0.1951, and TUEV, 0.3916 versus 0.4307. On ISRUC, training the downstream sequence encoder shrinks the apparent native advantage from about 3.40 to 0.24 percentage points.
- **Catch:** Only three seeds; designs are compared as complete modules, not a pure isolation of attention versus MLP mixing. TUEV subject disjointness is unverified, and CBraMod's pretraining exclusion of TUEV is undocumented. The main LaBraM sleep protocol freezes a newly initialized sequence encoder, so the follow-up is essential context. This does not prove EEG structure is useless.
- **Read scope:** Representation/adapter design, capacity matching, primary tables, splitting caveats, branch-preserving control, and sequence-encoder sensitivity. No full supplement audit.

### 4. **Conformal Prediction for Time-Dependent PDEs**

- **Classification:** Direct uncertainty/scientific-ML relevance; adjacent warning for learned world-model guarantees. **Verdict:** Useful lighter recommendation; theory-focused follow-up candidate, no detailed note today.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.08409](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08409), November 5, 2026.
- **Mechanism:** Treat PDE evolution as distribution shift, not automatic exchangeability. For a specified heat equation and Gaussian initial field, distributions at distinct times are mutually singular in function space. On finite grids, linear dynamics preserve Gaussianity, permitting likelihood-ratio-weighted conformal prediction; coarse grids or coherent regions of interest can avoid vacuous full-grid bands.
- **Evidence:** Theorem 4.1 establishes maximal total-variation distance even for arbitrarily nearby times under its assumptions. Experiments target 90% joint spatial coverage on synthetic PDEs, pulsed thermography, FRAP microscopy, and lake-temperature data. The paper reports Gaussian-weighted CP as the consistently covering method in the tested settings, while explicitly tracking infinite-width bands.
- **Catch:** Per-future-time marginal coverage is not simultaneous coverage over an entire rollout. Formal weighting guarantees require linear/Gaussian structure; nonlinear extensions lose them. Estimated covariance can under-cover when samples are scarce relative to dimension, and the proposed robust estimation correction is generally intractable. Coverage obtained with infinite bands is honest but not operationally useful.
- **Read scope:** Setup, main theorem/proposition statements and proof sketches, discrete-to-continuous lifting, experiments, and limitations. Full proofs and all appendix result tables were not audited.

### 5. **FisherDistill: Diffusion Distribution Distillation via Closed-Form Fisher Critics**

- **Classification:** Direct diffusion-method relevance; adjacent inspiration for explicit statistical critics. **Verdict:** Useful lighter recommendation; no detailed note today.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.07604](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07604), November 5, 2026. [Project/code landing page](https://bhosalems.github.io/FisherDistill/).
- **Mechanism:** Replace the extra learned GAN discriminator used in distillation refinement with a regularized Fisher direction in frozen teacher features: solve pooled-covariance times direction equals real-minus-generated feature mean. Real statistics are fixed; generated statistics use EMA and a one-step lag. Gradients pass through teacher features into the student, not through the statistics or linear solve.
- **Evidence:** On SD-v1.5/DMD2, full-covariance refinement reaches FID 8.46 versus 9.18 for diagonal scaling and 9.85 for mean-only correction. ImageNet-64/SiD improves from a 1.52 initialization to 1.34. The critic-lifecycle ablation matters: a legacy update scheme worsens FID to 1.63, whereas lagged updates reach 1.38 before delayed activation gives 1.34.
- **Catch:** No extra learned discriminator does not mean no learned auxiliary model: the base distillation stack can retain a fake-score network. Frozen teacher features, layer/noise selection, real-image access, covariance conditioning, and update timing remain substantive design dependencies. ImageNet's reported uncertainty includes evaluation trials, not necessarily independently trained models; improved FID does not imply all preference or coverage metrics improve.
- **Read scope:** Main refinement construction, online statistics, principal image/text-to-image tables, and covariance/lifecycle ablations. Full supplementary derivations were not audited.

## Most relevant to cabbageland

**Permanent and Transient Representations.** The useful decomposition assigns different inductive biases to different timescales: precise local correction should not require changing the representation that supports broad generalization. MinHash similarity, identity-tagged slots, consolidation, and decay are concrete mechanisms, not merely two networks renamed as memory. The boundary is equally concrete: value correction is not a world model, symbolic observation overlap is not learned semantic similarity, and fewer environment steps do not automatically mean faster training.

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

- **Continual RL:** A fast residual dictionary plus slow neural estimator is a necessary system-level baseline alongside optimizer/reset/regularization tricks. Compare at matched table capacity, tuning budget, environment steps, wall-clock cost, and observation preprocessing. The permanent–transient decomposition predates this paper; complementary representations and the slot-based approximator are the relevant extension.
- **Generative modeling:** Report quality–coverage frontiers, not one best FID. For Competitive FM, separate WTA from extra training, extra parameters, and guide-model choices; test fixed versus resampled codes. For FisherDistill, include mean-only and diagonal-covariance controls and audit when statistics are updated.
- **Neuro models:** An adapter beating a frozen linear probe establishes neither the value of its structure nor broad transfer. Use parameter-matched MLP controls, subject-disjoint splits, pretraining-overlap checks, and a clearly specified sequence head.
- **Uncertainty:** Name the random unit, grid/region, time horizon, shift assumptions, and fraction of vacuous intervals. A per-time coverage theorem is not a blanket guarantee for a continuous physical rollout.

## One-paragraph takeaway

Explicit structure earns its place when it controls interference, exposes a useful operating axis, or makes a claim falsifiable. The RL paper gives rapid corrections their own storage and generalization rule; Competitive FM lets multiple predictions compete without deploying an ensemble; FisherDistill replaces one learned optimization loop with inspectable statistics. The EEG and PDE papers keep those ideas honest: a structured module still needs an equally large simple control, and a guarantee still depends on what is being predicted and which distribution supplies calibration. Preserve the mechanisms, but carry their compute and assumption boundaries with them.
