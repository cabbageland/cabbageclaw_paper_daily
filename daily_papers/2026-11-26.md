## Ranked papers

### 1. **Guidance as Variance Matching**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant diffusion/flow modeling, controllability, and mechanism. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve. **Most relevant today.**
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.34801](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34801), Ali Backour, Artem Lukoianov, Frank Permenter, Tommi Jaakkola, Justin Solomon, and Vincent Sitzmann; [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.34801); [project and code](https://ottogin.github.io/vmg/).
- **Mechanism:** Fit a guidance scale by matching the total variance of guided clean-sample predictions to clean calibration data. With D(w) = D0 + w(D1-D0), prediction variance is quadratic in w, giving a closed-form global scale or a noise-dependent schedule. The proposed explanation is that finite-training denoiser errors follow a scaled posterior covariance, and guidance amplifies that spread. That explanatory model has assumptions; the practical rule does not measure the full posterior covariance.
- **Why it matters:** Guidance need not improve denoising MSE to improve generated samples. Calibrate a sampling statistic, then evaluate output quality and diversity independently instead of treating lower prediction loss or a single FID optimum as the universal goal.
- **Evidence:** Eleven released models span image, video, audio, motion, and world generation. The fitted constant is 7.44 versus published 7.5 for SD 1.5, and 4.14 versus 4.5 for SD 3.5 Medium. A subset analysis reports standard deviation 0.06 from 256 SD 3.5 Medium calibration photos. An informal blind SD 3.5 Medium study collected 1,458 votes from 41 people: its constant beats FID/FDDINOv2-tuned constants, and its schedule has the highest fitted Elo among tested schedules. Schedule gains are mixed across models and metrics, not a sweep of universal wins.
- **Catch:** Training-free still needs representative clean data and paired model evaluations. The mechanism idealizes retraining errors and substitutes averaging over inputs for averaging over retrainings; one fixed model pair has correlated errors. Matching a variance trace is not a general certificate of correct covariance, diversity, or prompt compliance. The paper explicitly does not explain all conditioning effects. The user study omits the near-identical published constant, so it does not show human superiority over that default. Negative-scale improvement is for selected model pairs, not arbitrary CFG.
- **Read scope:** Main method, posterior/error interpretation, main quantitative tables and limitations; selected calibration/cost, covariance-matching, and subset ablations in the appendix. No complete proof audit, image-by-image visual audit, or code execution. Project page resolves and links a public code repository. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/variance_matched_guidance_denoiser_statistics.md).

### 2. **Not all world models can doubt: how training objective and architecture shape epistemic uncertainty in JEPA and RSSM world models**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant world models, uncertainty, representation learning, and planning evaluation. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve as a diagnostic, not a paradigm verdict.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.34830](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34830), Simon Baur, Wojciech Samek, and Jackie Ma; [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.34830).
- **Mechanism:** Freeze LeWorldModel and DreamerV3 trained on the same recorded simulator data; fit last-layer Laplace uncertainty, with a five-head ensemble check. Perturb physical coordinates, re-render, and trace the change through encoder, state update, predictor feature, and uncertainty. Dreamer's recurrent update suppresses brief changes; the reconstruction signal emphasizes entities occupying more pixels. A head attached before the recurrent attenuation recovers much of the missed sensitivity.
- **Why it matters:** A stronger uncertainty estimator cannot recover distinctions already removed from its features. Audit where information disappears before adding an ensemble, a safety penalty, or claims that a latent state knows when it does not know.
- **Evidence:** Ten visual-control environments and five uncertainty-dependent tasks. LeWM exceeds Dreamer in 13 of 17 body/object unfamiliar-region detection cells and ranks its own prediction errors better in eight of ten environments. Physics shifts have no consistent winner. CEM planning with uncertainty penalties improves LeWM success in six of nine rare-region settings and five long-horizon settings; selected gains are 17–28 percentage points. The posterior-head ablation substantially restores Dreamer's violation/dynamics-shift detection.
- **Catch:** One model instance per paradigm and one frozen backbone checkpoint per environment; native inputs differ, 224x224 for LeWM versus 64x64 for Dreamer. Bootstrap/episode variation is not backbone-training uncertainty. The unfamiliar quadrant is withheld when fitting the uncertainty head, not when training the backbone. Error-ranking targets differ by family. Planning reports the best of three penalty weights and uses different goal costs. Reconstruction/pixel-share correlations are not an isolated causal objective ablation; causal retraining is future work. Multi-object and invisible-contact failures remain.
- **Read scope:** Main stage-trace analysis, detection/error-ranking/planning results, limitations, and selected appendix architecture, head, planning-selection, and posterior-readout protocols. Not every environment table or implementation audited; no model execution. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/world_model_uncertainty_feature_attenuation_jepa_rssm.md).

### 3. **Concept Generalization in Visual Brain Decoding via Encoder-Generated fMRI**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant neuro AI and evaluation; adjacent inspiration for model-generated supervision. **Verdict:** Useful; lighter recommendation with a concrete transfer recipe.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.33870](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33870), Peter Yongho Kim, Juhyeon Park, Dongsheng Li, Yansen Wang, Dongqi Han, Mingqing Xiao, and Taesup Moon; [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33870).
- **Mechanism:** Remove every image containing one COCO supercategory from a target subject's paired training data. Adapt an image-to-fMRI encoder on the remaining pairs, predict subject responses for additional concept-containing images, and train the fMRI-to-image decoder on real plus synthetic pairs. Use object recall alongside global embedding metrics to test whether the missing concept itself returns.
- **Why it matters:** Quantity and semantic coverage are different data shortages. A better-generalizing forward model can supply structured supervision to an inverse model, but the new image pool must cover the missing concepts and preserve image-response pairing.
- **Evidence:** Three decoders, four NSD subjects, twelve supercategories. MindEye2 object recall falls from 0.443 with concept exposure to 0.096 without it, then recovers to 0.288 with synthetic pairs; Brain-Diffuser goes 0.330 to 0.073 to 0.273. Subject-1 controls recover only 2%/12% of the recall gap with non-concept data/wrong responses, versus 64% with correct synthetic pairs; within-concept shuffling also removes most gains. Real responses remain better at equal budgets.
- **Catch:** This is target-subject concept holdout, not a concept unseen by all pretraining models: other subjects and visual priors can supply it. The 87%-versus-22% encoder/decoder recall-retention comparison uses different pipelines and does not isolate an intrinsic directional advantage. The main synthesis pool reuses removed training images, not test fMRI; a fixed general pool is an additional control. Four NSD subjects, detector-dependent recall, and concept-covered retraining do not establish open-world brain decoding.
- **Read scope:** Holdout definition, synthesis procedure, main decoder results, pairing/content controls, and conclusion/limitations; selected appendix control table. Not the complete limited-data/video/uncertainty appendix; no implementation run. No detailed note today.

### 4. **Diffusion Steering via Adaptive Reward Estimation and Resampling**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant generative modeling, inference-time optimization, and controllability. **Verdict:** Useful targeted method; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.34839](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34839), Abinitha Gourabathina, Sana Tonekaboni, Marzyeh Ghassemi, and Akbar Rafiey; [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.34839).
- **Mechanism:** TrailMix maintains parallel denoising trajectories, makes short clean-image lookaheads, scores them, and chooses the weakest exponential reward tilt reaching a target mean score. Resampling subtracts the reward weighting inherited from the previous checkpoint instead of repeatedly counting old selection. Finish trajectories and select the best final image. This approximates a reward-tilted distribution; four noisy previews do not make exact constrained sampling.
- **Why it matters:** Spend generation compute on informative previews and promising trajectories rather than completing every candidate. Measure denoiser work and judge work separately; particle count alone is not a compute budget.
- **Evidence:** On SDXL, GenEval rises from 63.0 for Best-of-4 and 61.4 for FK steering to 69.4; RareBench multi-object rises from 62.7/60.0 to 69.0. TrailMix uses 928 denoiser evaluations and 12 judge calls versus Best-of-8's 1600/8 and FK's 800/24. Best-of-8 still wins selected counting/color scores. The paper also tests semantic editing with a 316-pair benchmark and a separate 192-edit stereotype cohort.
- **Catch:** Better than four particles is not necessarily faster than best-of-N on a given device; lookahead adds denoiser work and judging can dominate. Two-particle results are unfavorable. ImageReward quality, inherited particle dependence, reward saturation, and output diversity remain issues. Independent benchmark evaluators reduce direct judge circularity but do not eliminate metric gaming. Editing uses generated source images with reusable initial noise; the fairness probe is narrow.
- **Read scope:** Main target/weighting algorithm, text-to-image comparison and compute accounting, editing setup, and appendix limitations; editing result/ablation discussion only selectively inspected. No comprehensive wall-clock or diversity audit and no code execution. No detailed note today.

### 5. **Carrying Feature Ablations Across Sparse Autoencoder Seeds**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant interpretability and reproducibility; adjacent inspiration for structured representation interfaces. **Verdict:** Useful evaluation protocol; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.34637](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34637), Karl Li; [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.34637).
- **Mechanism:** Match SAE decoder directions across seeds, ablate each matched feature using its own activation-dependent contribution, then compare the resulting final-encoder output changes. An output test accepts squared effect discrepancy at most 0.25 of the reference effect energy. A cheaper screen compares whole feature contributions at the edited layer, not decoder directions alone.
- **Why it matters:** A geometrically recurring direction need not be a reproducible intervention: its activation support and downstream propagation matter. Validate the actual effect before reusing an interpretation or steering operation across training runs.
- **Evidence:** Five SAEs per setting; each main setting scores 2,560 matched pairs. Pass rates are 304/2560 (12%) for DINOv2 layer 6, 1057/2560 (41%) for SigLIP layer 11, and 1790/2560 (70%) for half-width DINOv2 layer 11. Random-direction and shuffled-match controls pass none. The layer screen reduces initial scoring errors from 57/66/86 to 34/23/34 across those settings, but one false acceptance persists on an independent third image set.
- **Catch:** The 0.25 tolerance is chosen, not a semantic equivalence theorem. Intervals resample features from fixed SAEs, not new independent training runs. The layer rule is explicitly post hoc, albeit followed by fresh-image/feature checks. A small aggregate error rate can hide costly false acceptances. Output-vector agreement does not establish that feature names or causal concepts match; controls differ in reconstruction/sparsity, and single-feature matching misses split features.
- **Read scope:** Ablation/test equations, image split and SAE training, main pass/screen tables, persistent-counterexample and discussion sections. Not every appendix surrogate/subspace analysis audited; no implementation run. No detailed note today.

## Most relevant to cabbageland

**Guidance as Variance Matching.** Steal the calibration interface: express a controllable sampling statistic in terms of existing model outputs, fit it on clean held-out data, and keep downstream quality, alignment, and diversity as separate checks. Its explanatory assumptions are testable rather than another renamed guidance heuristic. The uncertainty study is the more immediately useful companion for world-model evaluation: move or redesign the readout before assuming the uncertainty algorithm is the bottleneck.

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

- **Diffusion:** Separate denoising loss, marginal prediction variance, full covariance shape, and generated-distribution metrics. Compare VMG against the released scale, metric-tuned constants, schedules, and matched calibration cost. Do not call variance matching posterior calibration without testing its assumptions.
- **World models:** Compare prior-feature and posterior-feature uncertainty at matched input resolution, capacity, context, and estimator budget. Retrain with altered recurrence/objectives to distinguish measured correlations from causal mechanisms. Report held-out penalty selection and backbone-seed variation.
- **Neuro:** Add semantic holdouts and concept-level precision/recall. Include fixed general synthesis pools, within-concept shuffling, equal-budget real data, and cross-subject exposure controls; a strong image identifier need not reconstruct the desired concept.
- **Inference-time scaling:** Match denoiser evaluations and judge cost, not only particle count. Report lineage collapse and independent output quality alongside reward improvement.
- **Interpretability:** Match contributions and intervention effects, not just feature directions. Screen cheaply, then validate important edits at the output, including false-acceptance rates and semantic checks.

## One-paragraph takeaway

A representation can be easy to score while preserving the wrong thing. Guidance improves samples while increasing prediction error; a world model's uncertainty misses a change suppressed by recurrence; a brain decoder needs concept-covered supervision rather than just more pairs; a particle sampler needs useful previews rather than just more particles; and a matched SAE direction may not reproduce an edit. The reusable move is to expose the intermediate statistic, state precisely what it does not guarantee, and test the final claim independently.
