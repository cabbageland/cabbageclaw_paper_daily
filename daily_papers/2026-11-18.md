## Ranked papers

### 1. **Sample-Aligned Decoders for Latent Generative Models**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant latent generation, distribution shift, and conservative model repair. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve. **Most relevant today.**
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.22458](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22458), Yu Lei and colleagues; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22458), [project](https://torpedo2003.github.io/SAD/), [code](https://github.com/torpedo2003/SAD).
- **Mechanism:** Freeze the encoder and generator. Cache actual generated latents and fine-tune only their decoder, matching decoded-image distributions to unpaired real images in frozen DINOv2 features. Separately anchor the new decoder to the original decoder on real-image encoder latents with an L1 loss. The repair sees the distribution it will actually receive, while the anchor preserves the interface it must still respect.
- **Why it matters:** Generator errors need not resemble random perturbations of real latents. Testing and correcting the downstream module's deployment-input distribution is a transferable alternative to retraining the whole generator or adding per-step inference guidance.
- **Evidence:** On identical evaluation latents, FID-50K improves from 2.30 to 2.13 for DiT and 1.15 to 0.98 for REPA-E. In the separate 10k-sample ablation, aligning encoder latents instead worsens FID from 13.32 to 13.59 and reconstruction FID from 2.63 to 7.70. Removing the anchor roughly doubles reconstruction FID. Training and evaluation latent seeds are disjoint.
- **Catch:** The 0.9 GPU-hour decoder update excludes roughly eight GPU-hours to cache 40k DiT latents. No extra inference cost is credible because the architecture is unchanged, but adaptation is generator/sampler-specific: a DPM-trained decoder worsens DDPM FID-50K from 2.76 to 2.82 without retraining. This repairs local appearance, not missing modes or incorrect structure. Several preference/CMMD results do not improve; the manifold and density-ratio story is diagnostic/local analysis, not an exact guarantee for the neural implementation.
- **Read scope:** Full main text, theoretical assumptions/proofs, implementation and evaluation protocol, sampler-transfer and alternative-metric appendices. Project and code README checked; no code execution or weight download. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/sample_aligned_decoders.md).

### 2. **When Anchors Shift the Target: Advantage-Gated Adaptation for Diffusion Reinforcement Learning**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant diffusion/flow post-training, non-agent RL, and objective design. **Verdict:** Highly relevant mechanism; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.23038](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23038), Pu Cao, Min Zhou, Ying Shu, Tiezheng Ge, Qing Song, and Lu Yang; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23038).
- **Mechanism:** Positive-advantage examples retain ordinary velocity-target regression. Negative examples use the first-order tangent of the concave signed loss at a detached moving-policy prediction, so repulsion does not grow with the live prediction's error. This gives the gate max(-advantage, 0). A separate frozen pretrained reference, matched at the operating classifier-free guidance scale, handles long-term retention rather than asking the moving copy to do both jobs.
- **Why it matters:** Stabilizing bad-example repulsion can silently displace good-example targets. Isolate those two roles before tuning a shared anchor coefficient; local linearization and behavior preservation are not interchangeable.
- **Evidence:** SD3.5-Medium, rank-32 LoRA, three tasks. Full AGA reports GenEval 0.96, OCR 0.97, and PickScore 23.57. Crucially, the matched CFG-free variant ties the strongest baseline's GenEval at 0.94 and slightly exceeds OCR, 0.94 versus 0.93. With the same negative branch, tangent updates on both signs collapse OCR to 0.34 versus AGA's 0.94.
- **Catch:** Full AGA also changes rollout/reference/evaluation guidance relative to the standard baselines; its headline advantage is not a gate-only comparison. The unique-gate result holds inside the stated quadratic-anchor family, not for every stable RL objective. One backbone, short LoRA adaptation, and learned reward/quality proxies do not establish general policy-learning superiority or human preference gains. Held-out quality metrics do not all improve.
- **Read scope:** Main objective derivation, retention mechanism, training/evaluation protocol, result table, sign-branch and policy-copy ablations, and guidance analysis. No code reproduction or exhaustive appendix audit. No detailed note today.

### 3. **π⁴: A Foundation Model for 4D Scene Geometry and Motion**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant explicit 3D/4D representations and modular perception; not a generative world simulator. **Verdict:** Useful architecture and training recipe; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.23144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23144), Yifan Wang, Weiguang Zhao, Ni Yang, Haoyu Guo, Junting Dong, Weinan Zhang, and Chunhua Shen; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23144).
- **Mechanism:** A shared DINOv3 encoder feeds separate looped geometry and motion decoders. Train geometry first, then freeze it and condition motion on its detached features. Predict correspondence in image space and analytically lift it through target-view geometry, rather than forcing a motion regressor to learn correspondence, camera placement, and scene scale together. Reference-free view handling avoids a privileged input camera.
- **Why it matters:** Decompose a hard prediction along an interface with a geometric contract: visible tracks land on reconstructed target-view surfaces. Weight sharing also frees training memory for larger view sets, rather than being merely a parameter-count trick.
- **Evidence:** Approximately 0.5B parameters for the full system; 343M for the geometry comparison. Across 16 geometry settings, average rank is 3.00 versus Pi3X's 3.10. On six Oxford Spires routes of 361–800 frames, ATE is 0.7337 m versus DVLT's 1.3885 m, with all six routes completed. Single-view average 2D AJ is 72.43, narrowly above CoWTracker's 72.42; 3D tracking is competitive, not uniformly best.
- **Catch:** Tracking evaluation uses ground-truth-based scale/alignment, so those numbers do not establish raw metric accuracy. One forward pass includes repeated transformer blocks, not one cheap computation. Motion/camera ambiguity remains; distant camera coordinates cause larger errors; long-sequence geometry still trails optimization-based approaches in some cases. Integration draws heavily on π³/Pi3X, looped decoding, and MoGe-style refinement. This recovers observed 4D structure; it does not forecast counterfactual futures.
- **Read scope:** Main architecture, supervision/data design, training protocol, geometry/tracking evaluation, selected decoder/refiner ablations, and stated limitations. No artifact execution or exhaustive supplement audit. No detailed note today.

### 4. **Retrieval-Augmented Correction for Generalist Medical Image Segmentation**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant medical AI and explicit reusable memory; adjacent inspiration for other repair systems. **Verdict:** Useful, with strong operating-regime caveats; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.23189](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23189), Vincenzo Marcianò, Xiaoming Zhang, Michela Antonelli, Sébastien Ourselin, and Maria A. Zuluaga; TMLR, October 2026; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23189), [code](https://github.com/robustml-eurecom/grace).
- **Mechanism:** GRACE retrieves verified same-anatomy masks and supplies an exemplar as a spatial channel to a shared refiner. A small projector on frozen MedGemma embeddings learns similarity against shape Dice. Train the refiner on mixed synthetic mask corruptions, retaining some clean inputs and occasionally dropping the exemplar. Compare pixel U-Net, deterministic latent, and flow-matching refiners rather than assuming a generative refiner is necessary.
- **Why it matters:** External verified examples can carry anatomy-specific structure without a new set of weights per organ. The ablations identify the useful memory as the actual shape exemplar, not primarily the foundation-model embedding or the RAG label.
- **Evidence:** Eighteen public datasets in total, including three absent from training and indexing. With weak upstream DSC 0.386, GRACE-U adds 0.353; on TotalSegmentator at 0.932, mean changes are 0.000–0.008. Held-out ProstateX gains 0.041 DSC for GRACE-U. The flow variant stays within 0.01 DSC of the deterministic latent version at about seven times the compute.
- **Catch:** Held-out dataset transfer is not unseen-anatomy zero-shot transfer. Adding an unseen organ requires 118–302 verified support masks. Held-out ultrasound loses 0.202 DSC; 3.8% of inputs above 0.8 DSC lose more than 0.05 after refinement. Some upstream prompt models receive oracle boxes, and the very weak PostDAE comparison applies a 2D single-anatomy prior outside its intended regime. A mask-only retrieval encoder is close to MedGemma: do not assign the gain to multimodal semantics without that control.
- **Read scope:** Main retrieval/refiner losses, split/index protocol, real-segmentor and held-out transfer tables, exemplar/encoder analysis, and limitations. Nonempty code repository checked, not executed; no complete cohort-overlap audit. No detailed note today.

### 5. **When Neural Collapse Fails to Route: Routing Drift in Continual Learning**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant continual learning and score comparability; adjacent inspiration for modular decision systems. **Verdict:** Useful targeted diagnostic; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.22429](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22429), Nicolas Larue; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22429).
- **Mechanism:** Separate task-local class discrimination from whether the global class argmax lands in the correct task block. Reliability-Calibrated Routing keeps the progressive equiangular tight frame classifier but mixes its standardized scores with radial plausibility around drift-transported, frame-shrunk class prototypes. Class radii have a shared competitive budget. There is no new task router and no test-time task oracle.
- **Why it matters:** Strong within-module accuracy does not make scores globally comparable. A replay buffer can look perfectly routed while unseen old examples lose to newer task blocks; audit held-out routing before declaring the representation forgotten.
- **Evidence:** Three image-classification datasets, replay buffers of 200/500, and paired frozen-checkpoint head comparisons over three seeds. On CIFAR-100/200, global accuracy rises from 41.71 to 49.74 and route accuracy from 42.81 to 51.43. Task-local accuracy changes from 85.53 to 87.28. The reproduced reference differs from published rows by up to 2.45 points, and the paper separates those comparisons.
- **Catch:** This is an ETF-specific post-hoc classifier correction, not a general cure for catastrophic forgetting or MoE expert drift. Prototype transport and radii still depend on the available stream/replay; scarce classes use fallback estimates. The known classes-per-task quantity sets the mixing weight. Evidence is small-image classification, not continual VLMs or world models; learned probability calibration is not established by the word calibrated.
- **Read scope:** Main diagnostic, transport/radius/scoring equations, paired evaluation protocol/table, component ablations, and scope distinctions. Supplement selectively checked, not exhaustively; no code execution. No detailed note today.

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

- **Decoder repair:** Compare against noise robustness, paired decoder post-training, a post-decoding pixel refiner, discriminator guidance, and contemporaneous FDDA. Keep official FID-50K separate from 10k ablation FID; include reconstruction, unused feature spaces, sampler transfer, and total adaptation cost.
- **Diffusion RL:** Factor sign-dependent feedback from frozen-reference retention. Match guidance, update budgets, and policy-copy schedules before claiming that a loss alone wins. A linearized prediction-space gradient is not a global parameter-space convergence guarantee.
- **4D structure:** Compare image-space correspondence plus geometric lifting with direct 3D regression. Report aligned and unaligned tracking separately; disclose loop count, peak memory, and optimization-based baselines. Perception is not automatically a world model.
- **Medical memory:** Compare correct exemplar, wrong exemplar, no exemplar, mask-only retrieval, and class-label-only controls. Show per-case harm and held-out modality results, not just the improvement of deliberately weak masks. Keep verified support data separate from the scored queries.
- **Continual decisions:** Report task-local, route, and global accuracy on held-out examples. Published baseline rows and paired reproduced checkpoints answer different questions. Neural-collapse-consistent scoring is a specialized design constraint, not a new universal learning theory.

## One-paragraph takeaway

The common lesson is not that everything needs an anchor. It is that each interface needs a specific obligation. A decoder should handle generated latents without forgetting real ones; an RL stabilizer should not move the targets of positive feedback; a motion module should respect reconstructed geometry; a retrieved anatomical prior should help damaged masks without damaging good ones; a continual classifier should compare old and new class evidence fairly. The useful papers isolate those obligations, test the corresponding ablations, and expose where the repair stops working.
