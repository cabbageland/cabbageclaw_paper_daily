## Ranked papers

### 1. **Stable probabilistic emulation of partially observed dynamical systems**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant generative world-model mechanism; adjacent scientific-ML application. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve. **Most relevant today.**
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.09317](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09317), November 6, 2026.
- **Mechanism:** Parameterize a stochastic interpolant's drift as bounded forcing plus energy-preserving and strictly dissipative operators. Skew-symmetric matrices cannot inject squared-norm energy; a negative-definite term supplies damping. This controls energy flow without projecting every state onto the training distribution's energy shell, so external forcing can change the stationary energy.
- **Evidence:** Train on 100,000 consecutive states and evaluate million-step stationary rollouts, forced mean/variance responses, and model-capacity sweeps. In the partially observed Lorenz-96 experiment, the model sees 36 slow variables from a 396-dimensional system. A 500K-parameter diffusion comparator becomes unstable despite having its family's lowest validation loss; the constrained emulator remains stable across the tested capacities. Under oscillating forcing, mean response remains useful but variance accuracy deteriorates later.
- **Catch:** The theorem concerns the continuous-time sampling process, not a certified finite-step Euler–Maruyama implementation, and stability is not accuracy. Geometric ergodicity additionally assumes bounded/regular coefficients and nonvanishing noise. Evidence is restricted to Lorenz-96 variants; dense operator costs make high-dimensional deployment unresolved.
- **Read scope:** Full main text, assumptions and proposition statements, Appendix C model/training/evaluation details, and selected forcing/sampler passages; not an independent proof audit. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/forced_dissipative_stochastic_emulation.md).

### 2. **Encoding Memory in World Models with Test-Time Model-Based Reinforcement Learning**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant world models, memory, and RL. **Verdict:** Highly relevant mechanism with a substantial runtime qualification; preserve.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.09625](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09625), November 6, 2026. [Author repository](https://github.com/burchim/TT-MBRL), whose inspected README still says code will be released soon.
- **Mechanism:** At each evaluation episode, reset world-model, actor, and critic weights to their trained checkpoint, then adapt them using episode observations. Mix training and episode replay 50/50 to regularize early updates; freeze the visual encoder/decoder and drop pixel reconstruction. Learn the policy in the adapted model, rather than treating a larger recurrent context as the only memory option.
- **Evidence:** R2I trained for 400M steps improves Memory Maze 15×15 return from 42.0 ± 5.8 to 54.1 ± 3.5, still below the reported human 67.7. That setting uses six runs; primary 100M comparisons use three runs and 100 evaluation episodes. On 2D 15×15, an extra-compute/training-data-only control barely changes R2I, 171.6 to 171.7, whereas mixed episode/training replay reaches 244.3.
- **Catch:** This is interactive test-time learning with rewards, continuation signals, retained training replay, and actor/critic updates—not frozen zero-shot inference or an explicit editable map. The reported R2I 3D evaluation time is 0.65 hours per episode with frozen visual modules, versus 1.04 hours unfrozen; these are both adapting variants, not a baseline speedup. Memory is discarded between evaluation episodes.
- **Read scope:** Main method/results, replay and freezing ablations, update-frequency study, actor/critic losses, pseudocode, and compute details; no code reproduction. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/tt_mbrl_episode_parameter_memory.md).

### 3. **Diffusion-free Score Matching for Exact Conditional Sampling**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant score-based generation and probabilistic inference. **Verdict:** Useful; priority follow-up candidate, not a completed detailed note.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.09758](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09758), November 6, 2026. [Author-linked experiment code](https://anonymous.4open.science/r/disco-code/).
- **Mechanism:** DISCO learns one score field for a slightly smoothed data distribution. Training still explores multiple noise levels, but draws its clean regression target from a lowest-noise posterior over data rather than reusing the point originally noised. Restricting a valid joint score to unobserved coordinates gives the conditional score. Image training adds partially noised masks to cover clean/noisy mixed inputs.
- **Evidence:** On CIFAR-10, unconditional FID is 3.18 versus EDM's 1.97; on FFHQ-64, 2.54 versus 2.39. Its stronger contribution is repeated conditional-resampling consistency: CIFAR-10 FID-10K drifts from 6.6 to 9.2 over 60 rounds, versus masked EDM's 4.9 to 15.5. This is less drift, not invariance.
- **Catch:** “Exact” means asymptotic sampling from a valid learned model conditional, not finite-compute samples from the true data distribution. The low-dimensional energy models permit Metropolis correction; image experiments use an unconstrained score that need not be conservative away from the population optimum, approximate minibatch posteriors, and uncorrected sampling. Scaling posterior construction and proving image-level joint consistency remain open.
- **Read scope:** Conditioning identity, objective and target-sampling construction, masked-training term, low-dimensional evaluation setup, principal image results, and resampling test; full proof and sampler appendices not audited.

### 4. **A Stochastic Latent Variable Generalization of Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant representation learning and predictive uncertainty. **Verdict:** Useful adjacent architecture inspiration; no detailed note today.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.09302](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09302), November 6, 2026; arXiv lists WACV 2027. [Author-linked S-JEPA code](https://github.com/giakoumoglou/sjepa).
- **Mechanism:** Add a context-and-target posterior and a context-only prior over a per-image stochastic code, then predict masked target embeddings with MSE plus posterior-to-prior KL. Retain the EMA target encoder and stop-gradient: the KL limits target-information leakage through the code, not collapse of the encoder itself.
- **Evidence:** At matched 600-epoch schedules, ImageNet linear probing improves from 72.9 to 73.5 for ViT-B/16 and 77.5 to 78.3 for ViT-L/16. Predictive variance correlates with error across pooled patches at Spearman 0.33, but only 0.16 within images. Reported scalar recalibration reduces regression ECE from 0.18 to 0.07.
- **Catch:** Main benchmark numbers are single runs. LeJEPA's displayed comparison uses 100 rather than 600 epochs. Uncertainty maps need repeated predictor passes, and visual completions require a separately trained diffusion decoder despite no decoder in representation pretraining. Error correlation is not reliable error localization or calibrated OOD uncertainty; this is spatial image prediction, not an action-conditioned world model.
- **Read scope:** Prior/posterior factorization and objective, architecture, principal probing tables, uncertainty analysis, compute paragraph, and conclusion; not all appendix ablations/proofs.

### 5. **Health-System-Wide Mapping of Demographic Information in Routine Clinical Histopathology**

- **Classification:** Adjacent medical/foundation-model evaluation inspiration; useful citation material. **Verdict:** Useful audit design, not a new modeling mechanism; no detailed note today.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.09851](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09851), November 6, 2026.
- **Mechanism:** Probe frozen pathology-foundation-model tile features with attention MIL across organs and demographic targets. Compare ordinary training, demographic-balanced exposure, diagnosis-context-constrained grouping, random grouping controls, and evaluation within diagnosis clusters. The transferable contribution is the control ladder, not another named backbone.
- **Evidence:** The cohort contains 1,302,440 slides from 257,322 patients across 25 organs and 69 organ/target combinations. Pan-organ mean AUROCs are 0.828 for sex, 0.733 for age group, and 0.662 for self-reported race. Signals remain under the tested balancing and diagnosis-context controls.
- **Catch:** One health system, no demonstrated external replication, and residual confounding remain. Sex/race splits are patient-level, but age splits are case-level, so repeated-patient separation is not established for age. Diagnosis clustering is fit over all eligible specimens before folds. Recoverability neither establishes a biological causal mechanism nor proves a downstream diagnostic model actually uses a shortcut; the literature-generated explanations are hypotheses, not localized image evidence.
- **Read scope:** Study/cohort design, labels, clustering and grouping controls, frozen-feature MIL, split/statistical protocol, headline results, discussion, and limitations; not the full supplementary organ-table audit.

## Most relevant to cabbageland

**Stable probabilistic emulation.** It makes structure do a specific job: constrain the generator's energy production while preserving freedom to respond to intervention. The best reusable lesson is to test both stationary behavior and forced response; a model can match the former and fail the latter. Keep the scope honest: a stable low-dimensional stochastic surrogate is not automatically an accurate physical simulator, a learned causal model, or a numerical guarantee for its deployed sampler.

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

- **Generative world models:** Distinguish flow constraints from fixed-state projection. Include unconstrained, projected, symmetry-aware, and parameter-matched baselines; evaluate rollout failure and intervention response, not only one-step validation loss. Stability and physical fidelity need separate claims.
- **Memory/RL:** Episode-local weight adaptation is now a necessary alternative to longer contexts. Match evaluation compute and interaction budgets, preserve training-replay access in the comparison, and distinguish world-model adaptation from actor/critic adaptation. Add explicit maps or retrieval memories before claiming parameter storage is the best memory representation.
- **Conditional generation:** Report joint fit, conditional fidelity, and repeated-query consistency separately. DISCO's energy-model theorem must not be silently inherited by unconstrained image scores.
- **Predictive representations:** Compare deterministic and stochastic predictors under matched training schedules; measure calibration and within-image error ranking, not only appealing variance maps. A visualization decoder is additional machinery.
- **Medical evaluation:** Balanced labels and diagnosis matching are partial controls, not causal identification. Audit patient overlap, fold-external preprocessing, institution shift, and downstream subgroup errors before interpreting a demographic probe.

## One-paragraph takeaway

The useful structure is a contract with visible assumptions. Forced-dissipative emulation constrains how energy enters and leaves a generator; TT-MBRL separates generic visual features from episode-specific adaptation; DISCO asks whether separate conditional queries agree with one joint model. S-JEPA and the pathology audit expose the corresponding interpretation traps: sampled variance can be poorly localized, and a detectable label can remain causally unexplained. Preserve the mechanisms and the tests that can falsify their stronger marketing claims.
