## Theme

Today’s good papers are about injecting explicit physical or structural context exactly where recent world-model and generative pipelines usually bluff. The strongest paper does not pretend a single occluded RGB view is enough for reliable geometry; it brings in proprioception and touch as hard geometric evidence. The best world-model paper does not treat embodiment as latent mush to be inferred after deployment; it conditions dynamics explicitly on morphology. The best remaining adjacent paper is useful mostly because it treats video–action alignment as a first-class generation target instead of assuming video alone can somehow become robot data later.

## Short overview

Brave search was unavailable in this run because the Brave API key is missing, so discovery fell back to direct recent arXiv API inspection plus arXiv abstract and HTML reading. I inspected the accessible primary-source text for the shortlisted papers, including abstracts and substantial HTML method/introduction sections, but I did not do a full appendix audit for every candidate. So this is careful triage, not fake certainty.

The strongest paper is **Physically Grounded 3D Generative Reconstruction under Hand Occlusion using Proprioception and Multi-Contact Touch**. Its core move is healthy: instead of treating in-hand reconstruction as a vision problem with stronger priors, it uses hand pose and tactile contact as explicit geometric constraints on a latent SDF generator. That matters because it turns occlusion from a purely generative plausibility problem into a physically grounded inference problem.

**Toward Hardware-Agnostic Quadrupedal World Models via Morphology Conditioning** is the cleanest world-model paper in the batch. The important idea is not “generalization” in the abstract. It is the refusal to hide robot morphology inside latent adaptation lag. The model gets explicit engineering specifications and conditions dynamics on them, which is a much more defensible interface for cross-embodiment transfer.

**VAG: Dual-Stream Video-Action Generation for Embodied Data Synthesis** is the best adjacent paper. It is not a deep representational advance, but it is solving a real bottleneck: most video world models cannot directly produce policy-training data because they do not emit aligned actions. Jointly generating video and action in one synchronized denoising process is a better shape than generate-video-first and pray an inverse model recovers the right controls afterward.

I also checked **PhysInOne** and **VLA-World**. PhysInOne is useful as a benchmark/data resource, but much of its contribution is scale and coverage rather than a new mechanism. VLA-World sounds relevant, but from the accessible text it still reads more like a familiar self-imagine-then-reason driving stack than a clean structural jump.

## Ranked papers

### 1. **Physically Grounded 3D Generative Reconstruction under Hand Occlusion using Proprioception and Multi-Contact Touch**

**Verdict:** highly relevant.

Why it matters: it injects touch and hand geometry directly into generative 3D reconstruction, then uses explicit contact and non-interpenetration objectives to keep the output physically plausible. This is exactly the kind of structure-over-plausibility move this repo should remember.

### 2. **Toward Hardware-Agnostic Quadrupedal World Models via Morphology Conditioning**

**Verdict:** highly relevant.

Why it matters: it treats morphology as known structure rather than a latent nuisance variable, and that changes the transfer interface in a principled way. Even if the result is bounded to quadrupedal interpolation, the framing is much healthier than generic “universal world model” rhetoric.

### 3. **VAG: Dual-Stream Video-Action Generation for Embodied Data Synthesis**

**Verdict:** useful.

Why it matters: it upgrades robot-data synthesis from video-only fantasy to paired video–action generation with a single aligned model. The paper looks more practical than conceptually deep, but the problem it tackles is real.

## Most relevant to cabbageland

**Most relevant:** Physically Grounded 3D Generative Reconstruction under Hand Occlusion using Proprioception and Multi-Contact Touch.

It earns attention because the extra structure is not decorative. The method adds new evidence channels exactly where monocular occluded reconstruction is underdetermined, and it uses those channels as explicit geometric constraints in the generative process. That is the right pattern: when observation is insufficient, do not just ask a bigger model to hallucinate better. Add grounded state.

The morphology-conditioned quadruped world-model paper is the best broader architectural lesson. If embodiment is available as engineering metadata, then burying it inside online latent adaptation is unnecessary and possibly unsafe. Explicit conditioning is the cleaner design.

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

The hand-occlusion reconstruction paper is good pressure on visually plausible but physically sloppy reconstruction work. It argues that under severe occlusion, realism is not enough; geometry has to respect contact and free space. That should matter for any paper claiming reconstruction for manipulation rather than just for rendering.

The quadruped paper is strong framing pressure on world-model transfer claims. If a model supposedly generalizes across embodiments, we should ask whether it truly learns transferable dynamics or whether it just infers morphology from motion history with a dangerous warm-up period. Making morphology explicit is not a cosmetic tweak; it changes the deployment assumption.

VAG is baseline pressure on the increasingly common habit of treating generated videos as if they automatically become control data. They do not. If the downstream task is policy learning, aligned actions are part of the artifact, not an optional afterthought.

## One-paragraph takeaway

The useful pattern today is explicit physical context, not extra vibes. The best 3D paper uses touch and hand pose to constrain a latent SDF generator under occlusion. The best world-model paper uses morphology metadata to condition dynamics instead of hoping the model infers embodiment quickly and safely from history. VAG is less profound, but it still gets one thing right: if you want synthetic robot training data, video and action should be generated together. The recurring lesson is simple — whenever a task is underdetermined, the right response is often to expose or inject the missing structure, not to celebrate better hallucination.
