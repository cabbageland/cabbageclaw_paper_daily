## Ranked papers

### 1. **Kolmogorov-Chapman Maps: Trajectory-Free Learning for Simulating Molecular Dynamics**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant stochastic generative dynamics; adjacent scientific-ML application. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve. **Most relevant today.**
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.12880](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12880), November 9, 2026. [Author-linked code](https://github.com/majhas/kc-maps).
- **Mechanism:** Learn a conditional transition-density flow whose time-gated Gaussian-reference parameterization preserves the SDE's infinitesimal behavior. Train a long transition to match two composed, stop-gradient teacher transitions using negative log likelihood. Known drift/diffusion supplies the local anchor; Kolmogorov–Chapman consistency links timescales. Peptide training adds a late detailed-balance term.
- **Why it matters:** A semigroup constraint without a local dynamics anchor could describe the wrong process. This paper separates those responsibilities and makes both explicit—a reusable design for stochastic simulators, not merely another rollout predictor.
- **Evidence:** Tests cover LJ-13/LJ-55 and three alanine systems, with learned transitions up to 100 fs. On AL6, the paper reports about 2.3× aggregate simulation throughput versus MD on one L40S and three of four TICA modes reached while MD misses two after an hour. Equilibrium comparisons match sampling GPU-hours; HFM training FLOPs are matched on AL4/AL6, despite different parameter counts.
- **Catch:** “Trajectory-free” means no paired-transition supervision: the peptide configurations still come from existing MD datasets. Known force fields, per-system training, force caps, momentum correction, and proposal retries are substantive dependencies. Sampling speed excludes training, compilation, and initialization. Rare-mode kinetics remain wrong; theoretical kernel identification assumes exact consistency and boundary conditions, not finite-training accuracy or exact kinetics of the corrected sampler.
- **Read scope:** Main text, objective and proposition statements, data/split/training/evaluation setup, inference corrections, and selected kinetics/compute passages; no independent proof audit or reproduction. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/kolmogorov_chapman_maps_trajectory_free_dynamics.md).

### 2. **Simulation-aided Reinforcement Learning with Control Variates**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant RL/control and trustworthy use of imperfect models. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.12643](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12643), November 9, 2026; arXiv lists NeurIPS 2026 poster. [Author-linked code](https://github.com/OmerHemo/Simulation-Aided-Reinforcement-Learning-with-Control-Variates).
- **Mechanism:** Subtract a simulated squared Bellman residual at real state–action pairs, then add its state–action-density-ratio-weighted expectation from abundant simulator data. The terms cancel in expectation under the assumptions while reducing estimator variance. Reset the simulator to real states, mask unreliable-overlap regions symmetrically, and estimate a scalar control-variate coefficient. Replace SAC/CQL critic updates, not the whole policy-learning stack.
- **Why it matters:** Simulation should not automatically get a vote on what the target environment rewards. Assigning it a variance-reduction role is a concrete alternative to trusting synthetic transitions as ground truth or discarding them entirely.
- **Evidence:** On Humanoid policy evaluation, CV-Qmix beats real-only across the displayed data budgets and overtakes ordinary mixing beyond roughly 2,000 real samples; that experiment uses ten seeds. Ant/Walker2D fine-tuning gains become clearest at large dynamics gaps. Offline Hopper/Puck Sliding comparisons average the last 50K of 200K update steps rather than choosing a best checkpoint.
- **Catch:** The unbiased-gradient theorem assumes the correct density ratio, independent sampling, and fixed target parameters; the implementation learns the ratio with a classifier and uses loss-level covariance as a proxy for the gradient-optimal coefficient. It cannot remove independently sampled real next-state noise. Shared accessible state/action spaces, simulator resets, and identical reward specifications are strong assumptions. “Real” means a second MuJoCo dynamics configuration here, not physical-robot validation; no blanket safe-policy-improvement guarantee follows.
- **Read scope:** Full main method/results, gradient theorem statement, masking argument, actor–critic integration, offline assumptions, and selected dynamics/compute appendices; no proof-line audit or code reproduction. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/simulation_aided_rl_control_variates.md).

### 3. **What kills v-prediction? A Patch-wise PCA Perspective on Pixel-Space Flow Matching**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant diffusion/flow mechanisms and architectural information routing. **Verdict:** Useful; strong follow-up, lighter recommendation today.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.12852](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12852), November 9, 2026. [Author-linked RiT code](https://github.com/THUMAI-Lab/RiT).
- **Mechanism:** Diagnose a conflict between retaining full noisy-pixel state for minor-component velocity prediction and using backbone bandwidth for semantic modeling. RiT gives a small nonlinear decoder direct raw-input access and adds a learnable raw-state transport shortcut alongside semantic features. PCA is a diagnostic intervention, not a required precomputed basis in the final model.
- **Why it matters:** Comparing prediction targets while silently changing their information paths can misdiagnose architecture failure as a fundamental defect in the target. Clean-data prediction already supplies a raw-input bypass through its velocity conversion.
- **Evidence:** In the unguided 250K-update study, vanilla B/16 velocity prediction has FID 149.07; restricting data and noise to PCA-128 yields 33.99. With a rank-128 backbone input, a width-384 raw-conditioned decoder gets 31.85, while removing raw injection yields 186.16. The final shared anchored shortcut reaches 27.97 in that ablation. Guided ImageNet results improve JiT-B/16 FID 3.66 to 3.01, with 137M versus 131M parameters.
- **Catch:** The evidence is class-conditional ImageNet-256, not text-to-image, video, or higher-resolution generality. Final model comparisons use separately selected guidance settings and are not equal-FLOP causal tests. Low-rank clean-data prediction reconstructs conditional means, not a complete conditional distribution. The minor-direction analysis and probes support the bandwidth account; they do not prove it explains every velocity-prediction failure.
- **Read scope:** Main PCA/probe/architecture analysis, controlled decoder and shortcut tables, model-scale results, sampling setup, and limitations; full appendix derivations not audited. No detailed note today.

### 4. **Good Views Are Not Enough: Global Counterexamples for Gaussian-Regularized JEPAs**

- **Classification:** Direct representation-learning/identifiability relevance; useful theoretical framing material. **Verdict:** Useful, with narrow theorem scope; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.12137](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12137), November 9, 2026.
- **Mechanism:** Construct view distributions where adding Gaussian marginal matching makes every scene-only encoder suboptimal: independently redrawn nuisance improves marginal shape enough to outweigh its prediction cost. A second construction retains this effect under exact whitening and a complete 512-dimensional scene basis. A separate readout experiment distinguishes geometric scene alignment from task-useful information and decoder access.
- **Why it matters:** Whitening, good positive pairs, and Gaussian-looking features are different constraints. None should be quietly substituted for a proof that the full objective selects invariant, task-sufficient representations.
- **Evidence:** The paper gives full-class exclusion arguments rather than just one bad optimizer run. On its separate four-state prediction task, at 512 labels and 1% observation noise, whitened prediction JEPA's linear NLL is 0.674001 versus NTP's 0.386775, despite substantially better scene MSE. Five encoder seeds and three shared-data repeats support that comparison; changing the task can reverse the ordering.
- **Catch:** These are specified finite-support distributions, Gaussian-discrepancy objectives, and noise/readout channels—not an empirical refutation of practical JEPAs or all identifiability theorems. The paper targets a uniqueness premise in the Gaussian extension, not the exact-whitening prediction-only result. Constructed counterexamples establish possibility, not prevalence; numerical certificates and complete proofs were not independently checked here.
- **Read scope:** Setup, main theorem statements and exclusion arguments, matched-readout experiment, implications, and conclusion; selected appendix text only. No detailed note today.

### 5. **Anatomy-Activated Mixture-of-Experts for 3D Medical Vision-Language Pre-training**

- **Classification:** Direct medical/foundation-model interest; adjacent structured-routing inspiration. **Verdict:** Useful, conditional on stronger reproducibility and capacity controls; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.10960](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10960), November 7, 2026. The paper links a gmum/A2MoE repository, but that URL returned GitHub “Page not found” during this run; public code/checkpoints were not verified.
- **Mechanism:** Predict crop-level anatomical composition, combine it with global image context and class-to-expert affinities, and share each stage's expert weights over spatial positions. Shared organ queries aggregate multiscale features for organ–report contrastive learning. Pretraining additionally uses pseudo-mask segmentation, composition, attention-grounding, and cross-stage consistency losses.
- **Why it matters:** This is a concrete test of whether an explicit intermediate state—organ composition—can improve representation routing. It also exposes an important distinction: fewer routing-logit evaluations is not fewer executed experts.
- **Evidence:** Pretraining uses 67,331 CT scans from 37,989 patients across public and proprietary sources. With downstream training, the full model reaches AMOS-MRI Dice 82.3 versus the SegMamba baseline's 76.7. A narrower routing ablation improves AMOS-CT Dice 86.0 to 87.5 and HD95 14.4 to 7.6. The study includes MRI, 3D ultrasound, classification, and detection evaluations, not only CT segmentation.
- **Catch:** All experts execute: this is dense soft mixing, not sparse compute activation or guaranteed equal utilization/organ-exclusive specialization. The full model has 104.9M parameters versus the 66.8M baseline; full-system gains do not isolate anatomical structure from capacity and auxiliary supervision. Cross-modality results involve downstream fine-tuning, not zero-shot transfer. A fixed anatomical taxonomy, proprietary data, promised releases, and retrospective rather than prospective validation limit the claim.
- **Read scope:** Main architecture/objectives/results, component/routing ablations, dataset summaries, downstream protocol passages, and generalizability/limitations appendices; not a complete per-organ supplementary audit. No detailed note today.

## Most relevant to cabbageland

**KC-maps.** Its strongest transferable idea is the division of labor: put known local physics in the parameterization and learn consistency across time at the distribution level. That earns “structured stochastic simulator,” not an unconstrained claim of causal world understanding. The RL runner-up supplies the complementary principle: if the model is imperfect, constrain how its information enters the estimator rather than pretending the model is reality.

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

- **Stochastic world models:** Compare local-dynamics anchoring plus semigroup consistency against consistency alone, paired-trajectory supervision, and deterministic maps plus thermostats. Separate equilibrium quality, transition kinetics, deployed correction effects, sampling throughput, and total amortization cost.
- **Simulation-aided RL:** Include ordinary sim-mixing, real-only, filtering, and control-variate critic baselines. Audit density-ratio estimation and state-reset privilege. Unbiased fixed-target critic gradients do not imply unbiased learned policies or monotone real-world improvement.
- **Flow matching:** Match input bandwidth, raw-state bypasses, decoder capacity, and guidance before attributing quality differences to velocity versus clean-data objectives. A conditional-mean completion theorem is not conditional-distribution recovery.
- **JEPA/representation learning:** Analyze the entire regularized objective, not only the view relation or covariance constraint. Report invariant alignment, noise-stable task information, and matched-budget readout risk separately.
- **Medical routing:** Use same-capacity dense and soft-MoE controls; isolate histogram conditioning from pseudo-mask/text supervision. Measure end-to-end compute rather than marketing router-logit savings as sparse inference.

## One-paragraph takeaway

The useful structure today is a contract over information flow. Physics anchors the transition family, composition constrains time, a simulator supplies a centered correction, and raw pixels get a short path around the semantic bottleneck. The cautionary papers show why contracts matter: a marginal Gaussian penalty can reward nuisance, and an anatomical expert router can still execute every expert. Preserve mechanisms with their boundary conditions attached. “Consistent,” “unbiased,” “invariant,” and “efficient” only become informative after specifying the object, assumptions, and computational path they describe.
