## Ranked papers

### 1. **FaST: Factored Synthesis of Transitions for Offline Reinforcement Learning**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant RL, structured generative modeling, and controllable data synthesis. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve. **Most relevant today.**
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.13013](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13013), November 9, 2026. Public implementation availability was not established in this read.
- **Mechanism:** Factor a transition into state, action-given-state, dynamics, reward, and termination. Fit each with an estimator suited to its statistical shape: transformed autoregressive mixtures, a bounded action mixture, delta regressors, a reward regressor, and a tree classifier. At synthesis time, redraw only the action conditional and retain the highest-IQL-advantage candidate before generating its consequences.
- **Why it matters:** Modularity earns its keep by exposing a useful intervention. Best-of-N changes action selection while retaining the generator's state marginal; filtering complete transitions changes that marginal too. This is a concrete baseline for controllable augmentation, not merely a named decomposition.
- **Evidence:** On twelve D4RL locomotion/maze datasets, untargeted FaST is broadly comparable to SynthER. Best-of-10 improves over real-data training in 11 of 12 medium/medium-replay locomotion settings across TD3+BC and IQL, averaging +8.9 normalized points. On one RTX 2070 SUPER, one million untargeted transitions take 18.6 seconds versus 1,736.0 for SynthER; training takes 128.7 versus 42.5 minutes. Synthetic results use three generation seeds and three learner seeds per generation seed.
- **Catch:** Expert-mixture targeting can lose 20–35 points; the shared learned advantage is a plausible common failure source, not an independently established account of every collapse. The chain rule is an identity, not a causal discovery result. Coordinate bounds do not guarantee jointly valid states, and best-of-N can shift inputs toward poorly modeled tails. Dynamics are ensemble-mean predictions suited to near-deterministic low-dimensional environments; independent transitions provide no multistep coherence. The speed ratio is generation-only, not end-to-end RL speedup. Offline-to-online refreshing is proposed, not evaluated.
- **Read scope:** Main text, per-factor parameterizations/objectives, sampling algorithm, downstream protocols/results, failure analysis, and selected compute/support/candidate-count appendices; no code run or reproduction. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/fast_factored_transition_synthesis_offline_rl.md).

### 2. **Is Your Robot's Memory Just a Clock? Temporal Shortcuts in Memory-Augmented VLA Policies**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant embodied-memory evaluation; adjacent diagnosis for other time-indexed memories. **Verdict:** Highly relevant diagnostic follow-up; lighter recommendation. The sole robotics/VLA item in today's five.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.14488](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14488), November 10, 2026. The paper links [memory-clock code](https://github.com/bennidict23/memory-clock); execution was not verified.
- **Mechanism:** Inject pauses, wrist fidgets, or air-grasps that consume time without completing task events. Compare recording versus hiding their frames, rescale timestamps without changing images, and retrain matched policies with and without time encodings. Both explicit timestamps and the slots occupied by null intervals can serve as progress shortcuts.
- **Why it matters:** Successful counting does not establish event memory when demonstrations run at a predictable pace. The useful contribution is a set of causal probes that separates remembering task progress from reading a clock—not another larger memory module.
- **Evidence:** The released policy's success falls from 47/50 to 16/50 on PickXtimes under pauses. A policy trained without timestamps still falls to 21/50 and 14/50 on the two counting tasks. A test-time event index at least doubles success under each tested null-event type and raises SwingXtimes air-grasp success from 13/50 to 43/50. Timestamp rescaling reverses the direction of early stopping versus extra repetitions.
- **Catch:** All experiments are simulated and null events scripted. The event detector uses simulator silhouette/contact/force information and mirrors the perturbation construction; it is a sufficiency test, not a deployable real-sensor solution. It can erase real repeated work, misses some micro-stops, and is inappropriate when elapsed duration is itself task-relevant. Most retrained variants have one seed; reruns can vary substantially. This does not show all VLA memories are clocks.
- **Read scope:** Main setup, interventions, timestamp/no-timestamp controls, cross-memory comparison, event-index construction, results, and limitations; selected appendix references only, no complete 38-page appendix or code audit. No detailed note today.

### 3. **DnNP: Denoising Input Uncertainty in Neural Processes**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant uncertainty quantification and latent measurement models; adjacent sensor/scientific-ML inspiration. **Verdict:** Useful; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.14424](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14424), November 10, 2026. The paper links [DnNP code](https://github.com/iSUPER-ML/DnNP) and a public air-quality dataset; neither was executed here.
- **Mechanism:** Add a local latent clean input to the neural process's global function latent. A pointwise variational denoiser samples plausible clean coordinates/covariates; the encoder and decoder operate on those samples. The ELBO combines target likelihood, measurement consistency, and KL terms for clean inputs and function uncertainty. Prediction marginalizes both kinds of latent uncertainty instead of plugging in one denoised estimate.
- **Why it matters:** Uncertainty in what was measured and uncertainty in the function are distinct objects. A larger output variance or a single corrected coordinate is not automatically an adequate substitute for propagating input uncertainty through a nonlinear predictor.
- **Evidence:** PM2.5 interpolation uses 34 urban sensors, chronological train/validation/test segments, and separate held-out test locations. With latitude/longitude/humidity, RMSE improves from 0.457 to 0.320 and NLL from 1.098 to 0.296 over five seeds. A second application covers 29 California pressure stations. On MNIST, high-noise reconstruction gains do not guarantee digit identity: at input-noise 0.5, DnNP digit accuracy is 0.123 versus the noisy NP's 0.157 despite better RMSE.
- **Catch:** The main theory compares realizable marginalization against plug-in models with fixed observation variance, not every heteroscedastic NP. Gaussian noise specifications/clean-input priors matter; pointwise denoising does not capture correlated sensor bias. Noise-level sensitivity and learned-noise experiments are reported on the 1D benchmark, not comprehensive field calibration. The real-data baselines are mainly standard NP; broader modern NP/GP controls and calibration/coverage metrics would sharpen the evidence. Both datasets are small and retrospective.
- **Read scope:** Generative model, variational objective/inference, theorem scope, synthetic/MNIST and spatial protocols/results, and limitations; no independent proof audit. No detailed note today.

### 4. **Few-Step Streaming Video Generation via Condition Shifting**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant flow/diffusion distillation and audiovisual generation. **Verdict:** Useful, with modest measured efficiency gains; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.14033](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14033), November 10, 2026.
- **Mechanism:** In the APEX streaming system, one network acts as generator under the original projected text and as fake-distribution velocity estimator under negated projected text. Alternate detached flow-matching fake updates with teacher-minus-fake distribution-matching generator updates, retaining Self-Replay Forcing, perceptual regularization, and the frozen reference teacher. The text sign selects a role; it is not a negative semantic prompt.
- **Why it matters:** A conditional interface can share parameters across an otherwise separate estimator role. But removing one network is not equivalent to eliminating its computation, the teacher, or all distillation overhead.
- **Evidence:** Matched training measurements reduce peak allocated GPU memory from 102.7 to 98.2 GiB per GPU and mean iteration time from 35.9 to 32.6 seconds. At three backbone evaluations per block plus a separately counted one-step spatial refiner, 70 paired 180-second videos yield 22 instruction-following wins, 47 ties, and one loss against S2-Avatar. A 320-case StreamAV-Bench evaluation shows instruction/aesthetic gains alongside weaker metrics.
- **Catch:** Motion preference favors S2-Avatar; human-study usability is unchanged at 62/70. Pooled AVSync error worsens from 1.319 to 1.341 seconds, and the interactive track's visual quality falls. Three NFE excludes refiner and KV-cache work. Separate two-/three-NFE comparison rounds and optimizer-state experiments do not isolate every causal factor. Negated-condition fake estimates need not be calibrated true conditional scores; the manuscript explicitly qualifies that interpretation. This is generative streaming evidence, not validated physical dynamics or planning.
- **Read scope:** Acquired full-text main methods, update equations, evaluation setting, tables, training measurements, and conclusion; incomplete appendices, no implementation audit. No detailed note today.

### 5. **IC-GNN: Modeling Individualized Brain Community Organization for Disease Prediction**

- **Classification:** Direct neuro/medical-model interest; adjacent explicit-structure and interpretable-readout design. **Verdict:** Useful; lighter recommendation pending reproducibility.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.13070](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13070), November 9, 2026. The manuscript says code will be released upon publication; public implementation availability is not established.
- **Mechanism:** Keep the same 419 atlas-defined brain regions across people, but derive subject-specific discrete multiscale communities with affinity propagation. Use community-aware probabilistic edge refinement and adapt shared regional readout weights to each person's community context. The prediction logit decomposes additively into atlas-aligned regional evidence.
- **Why it matters:** Individualization need not destroy correspondence. Fixed entity identities plus variable group membership is a transferable interface for structured representations; regional evidence remains comparable without forcing everybody into one topology.
- **Evidence:** Classification covers 884 ABIDE, 1,114 ADNI, and 28,602 UK Biobank participants. ABIDE/ADNI use site-grouped cross-validation. AUROCs are 0.7541, 0.8646, and 0.6062, respectively. On ABIDE, replacing individualized communities with training-set consensus lowers AUROC to 0.7465; removing hierarchy lowers it to 0.7168. In a separate affiliation-profile analysis, within-site shuffling removes the gain beyond demographic/acquisition covariates.
- **Catch:** Large total N does not make the depression result clinically strong: AUROC is only 0.6062. UK Biobank models share one fixed test set, so fold variation is not five independent test cohorts. Consensus-versus-individual improvements are modest relative to fold variability. Additive model evidence is not causal biological attribution, and atlas/scan/preprocessing choices remain substantive priors. Retrospective classification and selected-site replication do not establish prospective clinical utility or longitudinal disease progression.
- **Read scope:** Main architecture/loss, dataset/split protocols, main result and ablation tables, reproducibility/confound controls, interpretability analyses, and release statement; selected supplementary protocol passages, not an exhaustive per-cohort audit. No detailed note today.

## Most relevant to cabbageland

**FaST.** Its reusable idea is an intervention-ready generative interface: sample a state once, compare several conditional actions, then generate each selected action's consequences with separately testable components. Keep the baseline and the failure: retaining the state marginal does not make a learned critic reliable, guarantee valid joint states, or supply coherent trajectories. The next useful experiment is scorer-calibrated action selection at matched total generation-and-training cost.

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

- **Offline RL/generative data:** Compare a strong cheap factored generator against diffusion before crediting iterative generation. Match final dataset sizes, include best-of-N versus whole-transition filtering, and report generator training, critic fitting, and downstream training separately. Test stochastic dynamics and expert mixtures rather than only favorable dense-reward logs.
- **Memory/world models:** Add null-event insertion, timestamp rescaling, matched no-timestamp retraining, and redundant-slot controls. Track event sufficiency and real repetitions separately from compression rate; retain explicit duration where the task requires it.
- **Uncertainty:** Distinguish local measurement uncertainty, global function uncertainty, and label noise. Compare marginalization against flexible predictive-variance models and inspect empirical interval coverage, not just NLL.
- **Distillation:** Report network count, active calls, optimizer state, peak memory, and iteration time separately. Match teachers, refiner calls, and history/cache work; inspect motion and synchronization costs behind instruction-following gains.
- **Medical structure:** Compare individualized, consensus, and matched random communities while preserving the atlas. Use held-out sites and covariate-preserving nulls, report absolute performance, and do not equate decomposable logits with biological mechanism.

## One-paragraph takeaway

Useful structure makes a specific operation possible: redraw one action, exclude elapsed-but-unproductive time, integrate over a noisy measurement, select a network role, or align individualized groups to shared entities. None is a free guarantee. FaST still depends on its scorer and downstream model fit; event memory needs a real event detector; DnNP needs an adequate noise model; shared-weight distillation retains trade-offs; and brain communities remain predictive rather than causal. Preserve the interfaces, the controls that test them, and the conditions under which they stop working.
