## Ranked papers

### 1. **Linear Probes Can Guide: Training-Free Control of Representation Generators**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant diffusion/flow generation, representation learning, and controllability. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve. **Most relevant today.**
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.33037](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33037), Pengwei Wang, José Morano, Botond Fazekas, and Hrvoje Bogunović; [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33037); [project](https://penway.cn/lism/).
- **Mechanism:** A linear probe commutes with an ideal clean-denoising posterior mean, so clean-feature logits remain meaningful mean scores during a representation flow. LiSM separates request weighting from the response that spreads a correction into representation space. Fit-Path combines a fitted Gaussian covariance approximation with a finite denoiser displacement, using one extra forward evaluation instead of backpropagation. Independent image-space readouts expose probe hacking: satisfying a latent score need not change the decoded image.
- **Why it matters:** Representation quality can buy a reusable control interface, not only better FID. The valuable experiment distinguishes a readable score, a calibrated conditional probability, and an actual visible change; those are not interchangeable.
- **Evidence:** On 3,840 requests across class, attribute, foreground, and keypoint control, Fit-Path reaches geometric-mean adherence 87.2 with preservation 72.8 and hacking gap 4.7; noise-aware classifier guidance reaches 72.1/78.0/7.2. Fit-Path takes 8.5 seconds/image versus unguided 4.6 on the reported A100 timing setup. Against LiSM-adapted solvers, DCPS reaches adherence 82.9 and preservation 76.1 at 48.8 seconds. Transfer refits probes/covariance for MAE and SigLIP 2 without retuning guidance strengths.
- **Catch:** Training-free means no generator retraining, not no labels or fitted components. Mean-logit exactness assumes an ideal MSE denoiser and does not make nonlinear request probabilities exact. Gaussian covariance is approximate. Three main tasks retain generator class conditioning; this is not a general text-to-image result. Stronger global control sacrifices preservation. The headline uses heterogeneous external task metrics, not clinical or physical correctness.
- **Read scope:** Main derivation, weighting/response equations, all main comparison/ablation tables, limitations, selected appendix implementation, composition, and runtime details. Not every appendix solver derivation or qualitative image audited; no checkpoint execution. Project page resolves and links code/weights. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/lism_linear_probe_guidance_representation_generators.md).

### 2. **Reliable and grounded reporting of any computed tomography scan with FORA**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant medical foundation models, explicit concepts, grounding, calibration, and verification. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve, with a strong evaluation caveat.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.32823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32823), Sezgin Er and colleagues; [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32823); [code](https://github.com/forithmus/FORA).
- **Mechanism:** Self-supervised 3D ViT features feed anatomically routed multiple-instance classifiers for 1,028 hierarchical findings. Attention-weighted token logits expose additive spatial evidence. A report plan carries calibrated finding probabilities, locations, and available measurements into a LoRA-adapted writer, alongside visual tokens and evidence images. Report generation uses supervised learning and omission-sensitive DPO; no manually annotated lesions supervise finding localization.
- **Why it matters:** Put a structured finding-and-evidence interface between volumetric perception and prose, then evaluate classification, localization, and report completeness separately. This is useful architecture even when the word “bottleneck” overstates its faithfulness.
- **Evidence:** Training uses 304,962 CT examinations, with 36,619 examinations held out by patient. Internal macro AUROC is 0.918 versus CoLiPri's 0.705; external Merlin AUROC is 0.907 versus Merlin's 0.826. Top-focus hits are 69.7% for pulmonary emboli but only 27.6% across ReXGroundingCT chest findings. Internal report macro recall/precision are 0.215/0.285 versus NV-Reason-CT's 0.066/0.132. The public repository resolves; models were not run.
- **Catch:** Broad internal comparisons penalize unsupported findings and processing failures as negative predictions: the 0.21 AUROC gap mixes coverage with discrimination, not matched-label architecture superiority. Reports supply noisy labels, including uncertain-as-positive and unmentioned-as-negative. Evidence decomposition is not causal attribution, and the writer has a visual bypass around concepts. Localization overlap is not Dice; high relative-to-chance ratios conceal substantial absolute misses. One-network training and largely LLM-extracted/judged reporting evaluation do not establish clinical readiness. Full CT data are not openly downloadable.
- **Read scope:** Main results/discussion; cohort/split, label hierarchy, encoder and objectives, organ routing, evidence construction, writer training, baseline handling, localization/report protocols, and release statements. Supplementary figures consulted selectively; not a complete supplementary or clinical audit. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/fora_grounded_ct_reporting_concept_evidence.md).

### 3. **Load Balancing Neurons: Firing-Rate Control Sustains Plasticity in Continual Learning**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant continual learning and non-robotics RL; adjacent architectural inspiration. **Verdict:** Useful, unusually cheap mechanism; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.32691](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32691), Jan Robine, Marc Höftmann, and Stefan Harmeling; [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32691).
- **Mechanism:** Track each ReLU unit's active fraction with an exponential average. Add a stop-gradient threshold offset, increasing it by 0.001 when firing is below a target band and decreasing it when above; freeze offsets at inference. This prevents both dead units and always-on, effectively affine units without resetting their weights. The entropy argument motivates balanced marginal gates, but does not control all dependence between neurons.
- **Why it matters:** Regulate an observable failure mode before recycling features. This is a low-cost baseline to try before adding replay, reset machinery, or a new continual-learning architecture.
- **Evidence:** With normalization and weight decay controlled, a narrow 49–51% target improves over reset/CBP comparisons on continual ImageNet and class-incremental CIFAR-100. The CIFAR analysis averages 15 runs and compares against from-scratch performance on the same accumulated classes. Across six Atari games with five seeds, load-balanced Rainbow is best in four and tied in two among the four evaluated variants; it beats CBP in five. The value head is deliberately excluded from control.
- **Catch:** The controller is not architecture-independent magic: BatchNorm plus L2 stabilizes supervised use, while Atari uses LayerNorm. CIFAR retains an expanding accumulated class set; this is not proof of no forgetting when old data vanish. Joint activation entropy uses a Chow–Liu approximation, so balanced marginal rates do not prove diverse features or optimal plasticity. Six Atari games do not establish broad RL superiority, and transformer/long-lived foundation-model transfer is untested.
- **Read scope:** Firing-rate/entropy argument, exact controller, supervised protocols/results, Atari setup/results, and practical normalization caveats. Exhaustive appendix sweeps not audited; no implementation run. No detailed note today.

### 4. **EEG-World: Context-Conditioned Dynamics of the Watching Brain**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant neuro modeling and evaluation; adjacent inspiration for latent world-model assessment. **Verdict:** Useful, especially for baseline design; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.32825](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32825), Junxiang Zhang, Jiayu Zuo, and colleagues; [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32825).
- **Mechanism:** Pretrain a GRU on continuous EEG band-power forecasting, then add frozen audiovisual features through a zero-initialized input pathway. Six seconds of EEG initialize a latent state for ten seconds of open-loop prediction; per-person linear heads handle scale differences. New participants receive ridge-calibrated heads. Hold EEG history fixed while swapping candidate clips to isolate stimulus information from persistence.
- **Why it matters:** A brain-prediction benchmark can reward remembering the starting state rather than understanding the stimulus. Matched-history baselines, amplitude-sensitive skill, and stimulus-swap controls are transferable safeguards against inflated world-model claims.
- **Evidence:** VAEEG contains 2,584 hours from 78 people. On held-out clips, forecast correlation is 0.261 versus 0.233 for the strongest history-plus-video linear baseline, with a 3.3-second horizon advantage. With calibration, the frozen shared model beats that baseline in 23 of 24 unseen participants. V-JEPA 2 improves the isolated clip-locked score from 0.139 to 0.188, while overall correlation barely moves.
- **Catch:** Most predictability is EEG history, and adding video yields no net transfer gain on unseen people. Correlation alone hides amplitude errors; zero-shot transfer does not improve squared-error skill. The target is one-second log band power, not raw-waveform or causal brain simulation. Mostly fixed clip order leaves carry-over concerns. Pooled clip identification is post hoc, and single-view stimulus identification does not separate the nonlinear and linear models.
- **Read scope:** Dataset/design, recurrent equations and MSE training, history-aware baselines, split/metric protocol, transfer and representation comparisons, identification controls, and stated limitations. Full preregistration/supplementary audit and release execution not performed. No detailed note today.

### 5. **RetraQ: Manifold-Constrained Critic Guidance for Flow Policies in RL**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant flow-policy optimization and support-aware RL. Today's one robotics/VLA-centered recommendation. **Verdict:** Useful targeted method; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.32148](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32148), Xuanzhuo Liu and colleagues; [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32148); [project](https://2027ICLR.github.io/retraq/).
- **Mechanism:** Use a frozen behavior flow's denoising map R twice: its Jacobian filters the critic gradient, and R retracts the updated action toward supported behavior. All reported experiments use one refinement step. Refined actions can execute directly or become stop-gradient targets for flow-matching distillation/online RL. The geometric interpretation is local and approximate, not a certified safe-action set.
- **Why it matters:** Accurate values on behavior data do not determine reliable off-support action gradients. A generative prior can constrain both the update direction and its endpoint; compare these components separately rather than calling all gains better exploration.
- **Evidence:** Final online LIBERO success is 78.1/81.3/66.4 across Spatial/Goal/Object versus BC's 63.3/69.0/55.3, with three actor seeds. Real plug insertion improves from 55.0% to 76.7% in 60-trial test-time evaluations; online training reports 98.3% at 30 minutes and 100% at one hour. The real test-time critic needs 270 forked rollouts.
- **Catch:** Retraction explains most gains: Raw+R is already strong, and filtering margins are within substantial uncertainty. Real test-time superiority over FlowDPG is not statistically established. On OGBench cube-single, filtering mostly rescales the gradient and Raw+R slightly exceeds RetraQ, 99.0% versus 98.8%. Repeated offline distillation can degrade more than AWR/FlowDPG. JVP memory, high-dimensional derivative error, and fixed behavior support remain costs; real validation is one task.
- **Read scope:** Main geometric assumptions, map/filter/update, training settings, LIBERO and real-robot results, OGBench/component comparisons, and limitations. Long proofs and every appendix fork-data detail not independently audited. No detailed note today.

## Most relevant to cabbageland

**Linear Probes Can Guide.** The steal-worthy idea is an explicit, reusable semantic interface on a generative representation, coupled with an independent check that optimizing the interface changes the decoded object. Use Fit-Path, identity response, and adapted posterior solvers as separate baselines; report visible adherence, preservation, compute, and internal/external disagreement together. FORA is the stronger follow-up for evidence-bearing medical systems.

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

- **Generative control:** Linear posterior-mean scores are not exact request probabilities. Compare fitted versus isotropic uncertainty, identity versus denoiser response, and independent decoded-image metrics. Keep class conditioning and probe-label budgets visible.
- **Medical structure:** Separate output coverage from matched-target discrimination. Compare concept-only, visual-only, and combined writers, and intervene on evidence regions before claiming causal explanations.
- **Continual learning/RL:** Include threshold homeostasis atop matched normalization/weight decay as a cheap baseline. Track dead and always-on units, old-task performance, and feature dependence separately.
- **Neuro/world models:** Give linear models identical history and calibration budgets. Use squared-error skill and fixed-history stimulus swaps; do not infer visual understanding from overall forecast correlation.
- **Policy improvement:** Compare raw ascent, filter-only, map-only, and raw-plus-retraction at matched displacement. Include fork-data cost, JVP memory, repeated-distillation drift, and uncertainty on small real-robot samples.

## One-paragraph takeaway

An explicit interface helps only if its measurements survive contact with the output. Linear guidance can hack a probe; concept evidence can be additive without being causal; balanced neurons can still be redundant; EEG forecasts can succeed mostly by carrying history; and a critic can improve its own score outside supported behavior. The common design lesson is to expose the intermediate quantity, constrain how it changes, and test the final claim with an independent measurement. That is more reusable than another model with “world” or “agentic” in its name.
