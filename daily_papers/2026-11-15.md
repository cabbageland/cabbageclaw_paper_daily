## Ranked papers

### 1. **ISLA: A Coordinate-Free, Measure-Weighted Transformer for Boundary-Driven PDEs**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant scientific ML, explicit geometry, representation, and invariance. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve. **Most relevant today.**
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.17141](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17141), Peter Sharpe, November 12, 2026; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17141).
- **Mechanism:** Give each boundary cell its physical measure and prescribed condition; nondimensionalize using declared physical scales and an intrinsic boundary length. Pool learned slices using measure-weighted softmax. Derive moving anchors from those slices, expose only relative distances and inner products to learned maps, and assemble vector/tensor outputs from invariant coefficients and covariant directions. Interior queries read slices and a learned boundary kernel without writing into shared state.
- **Why it matters:** Mesh density is not physical mass, and a query set is not part of the PDE. ISLA makes both distinctions architectural. Its sharpest contribution is the separable test: remove measure weighting and lose resampling robustness; admit coordinates and lose pose robustness. This is a reusable design and evaluation recipe, not a new claim that all physics must be encoded symbolically.
- **Evidence:** On AeroZoo's 515 full-split test cases, interior-only ISLA reaches mean reference-scaled velocity/pressure errors 0.082/0.298, versus 0.294/1.292 for boundary-conditioned, scaled GeoTransolver. On DrivAerML, replacing measures with counts raises resampled pressure error to 11 times the reference model; adding coordinates raises rotated error to 21 times it while retaining stored-presentation accuracy. ISLA is second to GLOBE on AirfRANS, not universally best. A 28.2M-parameter control nearly matches the 67.5M model's AeroZoo velocity error on a matched step schedule.
- **Catch:** Exact interior equivariance needs an exact boundary read and additional geometric assumptions; production token sampling and tree clustering lie outside that theorem. Remeshing gives a convergence claim under refinement, not equality on arbitrary meshes. Critically, evaluating a tree-trained AirfRANS model with an exact read worsens errors 2.0-5.1 times: changing the numerical operator after training is not a free upgrade. Unseen boundary layouts can approach or lose to a free-stream reference. Automotive volume accuracy is sometimes worse than GeoTransolver; large 3D training needs over 80 GiB per GPU. No conservation, PDE-satisfaction, or universal extrapolation guarantee follows.
- **Read scope:** Main Sections 1-7, theorem statements and selected proof/assumption passages, architecture, AeroZoo data/loss/baselines, and selected cost and D.7 ablation/tree-angle appendices; not a complete proof audit or code execution. Implementation is promised for PhysicsNeMo, not independently verified as released. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/isla_measure_weighted_coordinate_free_pde_transformer.md).

### 2. **Symmetry in Time: A Representation Theory of Linear Recurrent Memory**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant compositional state, interpretable memory, and scientific sequence modeling. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; next preservation candidate, lighter recommendation today.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.17162](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17162), Anif N. Shikder, Ramit Dey, Jan Minac, and Lyle E. Muller, November 12, 2026; [HyperSSM code](https://github.com/anifshikder/HyperSSM).
- **Mechanism:** Analyze no-decay, transition-only recurrent state as an ordered product in an associative algebra. Under compactness and continuous-readout assumptions, the task group must be a quotient of the group generated by those transitions. HyperSSM uses real, complex, quaternion, or matrix blocks while retaining associative parallel scans. One quaternion preserves noncommuting rotations; a sign channel below a quaternion handles S5 with five real state components.
- **Why it matters:** Quaternion or matrix transitions preserve noncommuting products that commuting scalar transitions collapse. Width cannot repair an algebra that identifies operations the task must distinguish. A suitable readout can discard irrelevant cover information, such as the sign of a quaternion, without discarding the operation. The useful lesson is to specify what state must compose and what the decoder may quotient out, then test long sequences and inspect the learned transformations.
- **Evidence:** Training on lengths 3-40, the reported best-of-three group results reach 100% on A5/S5 tasks through length 1000. For S5 with 32 generators, cited PD-SSM and Gated DeltaProduct results are 1.07% and 1.23%, near 0.83% chance. A 12-parameter quaternion gyroscope model trained on 1-2-second windows reaches 26.5-degree error at two minutes versus integration's 30.9 degrees. A held-out-protein torsion-to-orientation task gives 23.7 versus standard geometry's 26.5 degrees at 64 residues.
- **Catch:** All reported experiments use the transition-only tracking regime; the commutativity argument is not a blanket statement that ordinary affine SSMs or stacked Mamba models cannot encode order. Algebra/block selection is informed by task structure; complex channels are snapped to validation-selected roots of unity, and group tables report best runs. Baselines are largely imported from earlier work, not a matched large-model study. Protein orientation reconstruction from supplied torsions is not protein folding; qubit calibration is not general quantum simulation. Compactness, finite lower-state assumptions for the depth theorem, noise/decay, resets, and language-scale usefulness remain boundaries.
- **Read scope:** Full-text main theory, HyperSSM definition, benchmark protocol/tables, scientific applications, certificate statement, discussion, and open problems; supplementary proofs and all-run tables not audited. No detailed note today.

### 3. **Dataset Distillation via Drifting**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant generative objectives, dataset representations, and diversity; adjacent to continual-memory compression. **Verdict:** Useful; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.16642](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16642), George Cazenavette, Angelina Quan, Giannis Daras, Antonio Torralba, and Vincent Sitzmann, November 12, 2026; NeurIPS 2026; [project](https://drifting-distillation.github.io).
- **Mechanism:** Optimize a small class-conditional image set directly, not a generator or classifier. In a frozen encoder's feature space, attract augmented synthetic views toward real samples and repel them from other synthetic views, including other augmentations of the same image. Regress to stop-gradient drifted feature targets. A progressively grown Laplacian pyramid parameterizes the pixels.
- **Why it matters:** Pull synthetic feature views toward real data and repel them from one another while optimizing the image pixels. The represented object is the image set together with its augmentation distribution, not ten isolated pictures. Training the support of that distribution can preserve modes that decision-boundary inversion ignores. Distinguish matching distributions from distilling a teacher's changing predictions: the paper evaluates hard labels, one-time soft labels, and online knowledge distillation separately.
- **Evidence:** With noise initialization and MoCo-v3 features, 10-image-per-class ImageNet data train ResNet-18 to 27.0% hard-label accuracy versus G-VBSM's 11.7%; fixed-soft-label accuracy is 40.3% versus 29.8%. DINOv3-based data reach 77.0% coverage in held-out SigLIP2 space versus at most 9.7% for noise-initialized baselines, using five-neighbor neighborhoods and augmented views. Removing distillation-time augmentation collapses the default ablation to 0.2% hard-label accuracy and 1.0% coverage.
- **Catch:** The encoder and augmentations define what counts as coverage; high coverage is not full pixel-distribution fidelity. Feature-space comparisons include pretrained-model knowledge, and self-evaluations inflate coverage, so the authors exclude those cells from rankings. Most evaluation centers on ImageNet and a ResNet-18 student; audio/video extensions are not equivalent broad validation. Ten images per class cost roughly 250 GPU-hours for ImageNet before sweeps and extra backbones. Noise initialization is not a privacy guarantee. No demonstrated continual-learning retention benefit follows from the memory-compression analogy.
- **Read scope:** Full-text main distribution/augmentation formulation, drift loss, pyramid parameterization, primary classification/coverage results, and selected augmentation and compute/limitations appendices; not an exhaustive artifact or cross-modality audit. No detailed note today.

### 4. **Bellman Contraction under MMD: A Unified Framework**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant non-agent RL, distributional evaluation, uncertainty, and objective design. **Verdict:** Useful theory reference; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.17116](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17116), Baptiste Debes and Tinne Tuytelaars, November 12, 2026; NeurIPS 2026; [code](https://github.com/BaptisteDebes/BellmanContractionMMD).
- **Mechanism:** Reduce fixed-policy distributional Bellman contraction to conditional positive definiteness of the difference kernel tau*k(x,y)-k(q+gamma*x,q+gamma*y). For radial kernels, use Bernstein profiles to obtain analytic or numerical tests. An infinite chained RBF construction supplies a chosen squared-MMD contraction factor between gamma squared and one.
- **Why it matters:** The difference-kernel definiteness test exposes which discrepancies actually contract. A faster contraction can result from making a discrepancy blind, not from learning the return law better. In the studied Bernstein family, attaining the optimal gamma-squared factor collapses the discrepancy to comparing means. Choose a return-distribution metric for discriminative power as well as stability, rather than treating the contraction number as a leaderboard.
- **Evidence:** The framework recovers power-distance/multiquadric results, numerically identifies 11 additional kernel families, and constructs chained kernels with a prescribed factor. Synthetic two-sample tests show much stronger contraction/detection trade-offs for same-mean variance or modality differences than for mean shifts; additional tests reach dimension 64. Finite-set tests suggest RBF contraction may hold on sufficiently small bounded supports relative to lengthscale.
- **Catch:** This is fixed-policy evaluation, not control or a neural-training convergence guarantee. The factors refer to squared MMD; ordinary MMD factors are their square roots. Passing random finite-set tests is not a proof of contraction on an entire ball, and the 11-family search is numerical. Infinite chaining requires truncation. No RL-environment return improvement is established; the authors explicitly leave that experiment for future work. The detectability trade-off is distribution- and family-dependent, not a universal monotone empirical law.
- **Read scope:** Full-text main setup, kernel criterion, radial reduction, bounded-support experiment, chaining construction, trade-off statements/results, and conclusion; not the 88-page manuscript's complete appendix/proof audit. No detailed note today.

### 5. **Joint Null-Space Projection for Data-Free Model Merging**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant modular adaptation and interference; adjacent inspiration for continual learning. **Verdict:** Useful bounded mechanism, not a universal merging recipe; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.17103](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17103), Tian Xia, November 12, 2026; [code](https://github.com/sumorday/JNSP).
- **Mechanism:** For each eligible weight matrix, find each same-initialization task delta's top-r right-singular directions. Project that full delta off the uncompressed union of its peers' directions, then sum the projected deltas into the base. Every task sees the same peer set, making the rule order-independent. This is a conflict map, not truncation to the task's own top-r approximation.
- **Why it matters:** Project each full task delta off its peers’ dominant input subspaces. This symmetric leave-one-out filter makes a concrete alternative to entrywise sign voting or sequential deletion. But overlap is only a proxy for conflict: a shared direction may be useful to everyone. The unusually useful part is the paper's negative language result, which prevents a clean geometric picture from being mistaken for a general theory of function preservation.
- **Evidence:** Eight-task CLIP ViT-B/16 average accuracy is 89.45% versus TIES 77.57%, the harness's TSV-M/AlphaMerging 87.22%, and WUDI 88.47%; a second checkpoint release gives 89.20%. A shared-basis control gets 86.51%. At matched rank eight, sequential projection gets 69.64% versus joint projection's 88.03%. On eight RoBERTa-large GLUE checkpoints, however, JNSP gets 31.43 versus task arithmetic's 59.70; unscaled task arithmetic is 39.76, so projection and scaling both matter.
- **Catch:** Rank 16 was selected on the same eight-task ViT-B/16 sweep, so data-free describes applying the operator, not a fully selection-free result. Evaluation uses task-specific heads and common-initialization experts, not unknown-task routing or arbitrary pretrained models. WUDI merges 48 tensors versus 53 for most other rows; modern subspace baselines are reimplementations with narrower margins than TIES. Bootstrap intervals measure test sampling, not expert-training variation. No functional guarantee makes the deleted directions dispensable; peer-union saturation is a concern as tasks grow.
- **Read scope:** Full-text main operator/least-squares distinction, evaluation setup, primary/backbone results, rank/shared/sequential controls, and language failure analysis; appendices and code not audited. No detailed note today.

## Most relevant to cabbageland

**ISLA.** Keep the distinction between the physical problem and its numerical presentation. Measure-aware pooling, moving invariant anchors, and read-only queries address three independent sources of accidental dependence. Just as importantly, keep the exact-operator theorem separate from the approximate operator actually trained: evaluating an approximate-trained model with a more exact read can make it worse.

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

- **Scientific representations:** Include pose, units, resampling, and query-chunking interventions alongside ordinary held-out accuracy. Compare against augmented, boundary-conditioned, nondimensionalized baselines, not only released defaults. Match model size, sampling budget, and numerical read operator.
- **Compositional memory:** Distinguish transition-only group tracking from general affine recurrence. Compare quaternion blocks with real matrix/orthogonal alternatives, report all seeds, and test noise, decay, resets, and unknown task structure before projecting formal-language exactness onto LMs.
- **Generative data compression:** Treat augmentations as part of the learned representation. Separate hard-label learning, fixed teacher labels, and online teacher access; evaluate coverage in independent spaces and include synthesis cost. Drifting and dataset distribution matching predate this application.
- **Distributional RL:** Report contraction and return-law sensitivity together. Include same-mean/different-tail tests, squared-versus-unsquared metric conventions, support assumptions, and actual policy-evaluation/control results before claiming a better RL algorithm.
- **Model merging:** Use modern subspace baselines, held-out rank selection, task scaling, and expert-training variation. Test shared-beneficial directions and task-count growth. Right-side leave-one-out peer filtering is the construction to isolate; null-space projection itself is not new.

## One-paragraph takeaway

The theme is not that more explicit mathematics automatically wins. It is that each representation preserves some distinctions and erases others. ISLA erases presentation choices while retaining boundary relations; HyperSSM retains noncommuting composition; drifting allocates a tiny dataset across augmented feature-space modes; MMD kernel design can erase the very return differences it purports to model; JNSP can erase beneficial shared updates. The strongest experiments intervene on the specific distinction the method claims to preserve. Keep those experiments, and keep the failures next to the headline.
