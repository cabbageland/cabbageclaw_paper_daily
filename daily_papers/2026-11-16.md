## Ranked papers

### 1. **Restoring Diversity in Distilled Image and Video Generators by Probing Activations**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant diffusion distillation, representation diagnostics, and controllability. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve. **Most relevant today.**
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.18550](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18550), Furkan Horoz and colleagues, November 13, 2026; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18550).
- **Mechanism:** Measure activation covariance across seeds at every block. Distilled students retain total variance but concentrate it in fewer directions. ReDiverse-FT trains prefix LoRA adapters with paired-noise MSE to a later-step teacher activation at one diagnosed block, activating adapters only at the student's first step. ReDiverse-TF instead adds random, teacher-spread-scaled perturbations along prompt-specific teacher principal directions at that block and step. They can be combined.
- **Why it matters:** Diagnose which variation disappeared before injecting more noise. The teacher-direction versus student-direction versus equal-energy random-direction control is the useful experiment: diversity alone can reward damaged samples, whereas a good intervention restores meaningful variation without excessive quality loss.
- **Evidence:** Across SDXL, SD3, Wan 2.1, and MiniMax-H3 four-step students, the reported participation-ratio ratios are 0.37-0.60 while total-variance ratios are 1.03-2.92. On SD3, combined ReDiverse raises DINO diversity from 48.4 to 60.1, versus teacher 61.6. On Wan, it raises DINO diversity from 31.6 to 52.2, versus teacher 51.6. Image evaluation uses 553 prompts and 100 seeds each; video uses 300 prompts and 10 seeds each.
- **Catch:** This is not a free recovery. SD3 ImageReward drops from 1.026 to 0.872; video realism sometimes worsens. Training-free still needs 25 teacher trajectories per new prompt to estimate eight directions. The method assumes matching teacher/student architectures. Participation ratio is effective dimensionality, not a proof that a fixed percentage of semantic modes is missing; nonlinear downstream maps complicate that inference. Broad metrics support the intervention more strongly than a universal single-block-collapse theory.
- **Read scope:** Full main text; selected Appendices A, D, E.1/E.7 and J, including first-step covariance measurement, training recipe, random-direction controls, and human-study scope. No code execution or complete appendix audit. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/rediverse_activation_directions_diffusion_distillation.md).

### 2. **JVT: Just Video Transformer makes pixel-space video diffusion simple**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant video flow modeling, explicit geometry, and architecture simplification. **Verdict:** Highly relevant follow-up; lighter recommendation today.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.18549](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18549), Jianjun Zhou, Hua Geng, and Haoyu Guo, November 13, 2026; [project](https://zhoutimemachine.github.io/JVT).
- **Mechanism:** Predict clean raw video under a rectified-flow velocity loss with a plain DiT-style backbone. Factor patch embedding into spatial then temporal operations with a bottleneck. For camera control, concatenate RGB and square-root disparity on the same grid and jointly generate them; unproject into colored point clouds afterward. No pretrained autoencoder or separate inference-time reconstruction model is required.
- **Why it matters:** A shared pixel grid makes the geometry interface explicit rather than forcing non-RGB signals through an RGB tokenizer. Preserve the clean-data-prediction/velocity-loss distinction, and separate architectural simplicity from a claim that every component is cheaper.
- **Evidence:** Class-conditioned UCF-101 FVD is 136.5, or 129.2 with register guidance. On eight-frame RealEstate10K, adding disparity changes FVD from 27.0 to 27.5 but raises translation accuracy at five degrees from 72.4 to 79.5 and AUC from 52.8 to 58.6. Register-free models remain viable; registers without register guidance do not improve the default ablation.
- **Catch:** Standard generation is 16 frames at 256 square, not frontier long-video validation. Several baseline scores are imported, and UCF uses both train and test splits under the cited generation protocol. Pi3X supplies depth pseudo-labels and estimates evaluation poses, so geometry evidence is not independent ground truth. Register guidance increases denoiser evaluations; large patch-embed savings barely change whole-denoiser FLOPs. Generated point clouds are not a verified physical world model.
- **Read scope:** Main formulation, patch/register design, benchmark and ablation tables, and camera-conditioned method/results; selected appendix configuration references, not an exhaustive supplement or implementation audit. No detailed note today.

### 3. **Sample-Efficient Control of Magnetoconvective Flows via Differentiable Simulation**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant non-agent RL/control, differentiable physics, and system identification. **Verdict:** Useful mechanism and benchmark; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.18008](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18008), Jannis Becktepe, Dmitry Krasnov, Nils Thuerey, Jörg Schumacher, and Sebastian Peitz, November 13, 2026; [solver](https://github.com/upb-sciml/phiPICT).
- **Mechanism:** Extend a differentiable incompressible-flow solver with electric-potential, current-density, Lorentz-force, and custom adjoint operations for low-magnetic-Reynolds-number flows. Train feedback policies through ten-step simulator gradients; compare BPTT, short-horizon actor-critic, and SAC. A separate inverse problem optimizes 24 wall-conductance patches from sparse simulated velocity probes.
- **Why it matters:** Make the simulator's derivative interface part of the baseline choice. A strong physical model with usable gradients can beat a more elaborate RL learner; direct BPTT outperforming SHAC is more informative than another branded policy architecture.
- **Evidence:** Five training seeds and ten test episodes; training accounts for backward/checkpointing work using a three-times forward-equivalent cost. BPTT converges around 1,000 forward-equivalent steps and beats SAC/SHAC within 10,000. Lower-regime heat transfer rises over 25%; penalized IQM rewards are 0.236 for BPTT versus 0.133 SAC and 0.146 SHAC. In the inverse problem, L-BFGS-B reaches roughly three orders lower objective than CMA-ES within 30 minutes.
- **Catch:** Simulation-only, two control regimes, a small initial-state collection, and low-Rm/static-field assumptions. SAC uses default hyperparameters, weakening any broad algorithm-ranking claim. Forward-equivalent steps are a compute proxy, not a complete wall-clock comparison. The inverse objective ratio is not a thousandfold real-world identification-accuracy guarantee. Single CUDA GPU, demanding 3D grids, and simulator mismatch remain limitations.
- **Read scope:** Main solver/adjoint explanation, control observations/reward/training/results, inverse-problem setup/results, and limitations; gradient derivations and full appendix not audited. No detailed note today.

### 4. **AG-JEPA: Anatomically Guided Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture for Neurodegenerative Representation Learning**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant neuro AI, structured representations, and latent progression. **Verdict:** Useful; lighter recommendation pending stronger reproducibility.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.18097](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18097), Azad Singh, Ayantika Das, and colleagues, November 13, 2026; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.18097).
- **Mechanism:** Add tokens for five bilateral anatomical regions to a brain-MRI JEPA. Couple patch prediction, anatomy-to-patch prediction, patch-to-anatomy prediction, pooled-region alignment, and supervised attention localization. FastSurfer-derived region coverage also guides masking. A separate head predicts smooth-L1 latent displacements conditioned on interval, age, and current regional volumes, instead of synthesizing future MRIs.
- **Why it matters:** Named tokens need obligations, not just names: require them to reconstruct information, be recoverable from observations, and correspond to known regions. The frozen structured-state-plus-delta pattern is transferable beyond neuroimaging, but anatomical attention is not causal explanation.
- **Evidence:** ADNI subject-disjoint pretraining/probing uses 2,523 scans from 540 subjects; testing uses 629 scans from 135. CN/MCI AUROC is 0.804 versus Neuro-JEPA's 0.720; stable/progressive MCI is 0.689 versus 0.628. ADNI-trained probes transfer to MR-RATE, AIBL, and MIRIAD. Future CN/AD AUROC is 0.844 versus BrLP's 0.827; MMSE change is slightly worse than AG-LDM, not a universal win.
- **Catch:** The paper explicitly does not release code. MRI-only anatomy-guided pretraining is not a broadly multimodal clinical foundation model. The strongest early-stage result is within ADNI; external comparisons mainly test CN/AD. MR-RATE labels come from rules over radiology reports, not uniform adjudicated clinical endpoints. Repeated scans are not independent patients. Segmentation is extra training supervision, and progression conditioning includes regional volumes even though encoder inference itself needs only MRI. No clinical calibration or prospective utility follows from AUROC.
- **Read scope:** Main architecture/objectives, diagnosis/progression tables, training details, and selected cohort/report-label appendix passages; not a full statistical or clinical audit. No detailed note today.

### 5. **TelePhysica: Agentic Reconstruction of Physically Interactive Scenes**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant explicit physical state and agentic real-to-sim; adjacent inspiration for robotics. **Verdict:** Useful systems design, evidence-limited; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.18078](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18078), Ruiqi Shu, Xufeng Zhan, Yuanzhi Liang, Haibin Huang, Chi Zhang, and Xuelong Li, November 13, 2026; [project](https://interactive4d.vercel.app/).
- **Mechanism:** A coding agent builds a procedural scene from one photograph. First repair visual masks/contours; then compile colliders and check one second of zero-actuation gravitational equilibrium. Feed failures back for revision. Keep simulator state separate from rendered appearance, support rigid/cloth/solid dynamics and checkpointed interventions, and use simulator rollouts to condition pretrained video generators.
- **Why it matters:** A scene can pass a 2D render test while floating or intersecting in 3D. Independent visual and mechanical gates are a reusable real-to-sim acceptance interface; neither a video-quality score nor an agent's self-description substitutes for executable state checks.
- **Evidence:** On 100 tabletop images, closed-loop equilibrium acceptance is 81%, versus 0% for the first submission, with median seven scene submissions. But only 49% pass a full five-second forced rollout. Deformable reconstruction has three examples, two autonomously passing submillimeter rest drift. Video evaluation spans seven main interactions plus two counterfactuals, 53 generated videos in total.
- **Catch:** The first-pass baseline is very weak: 94% initially fail topology/binding or script/render execution. More repair budget is confounded with the benefit of physical critique. Mass, friction, and stiffness come from priors, not measured system identification. Motion scores compare against the simulator's own trajectories, not real interaction ground truth; matching that teacher does not establish the reconstructed world's correctness. This is not demonstrated sim-trained policy improvement or transfer back to a robot.
- **Read scope:** Main executable-world method, reconstruction table/failure analysis, video protocol, and conclusion/parameter limitation; no solver/code execution or exhaustive per-scene ledger audit. No detailed note today.

## Most relevant to cabbageland

**ReDiverse.** Keep the diagnostic-plus-intervention recipe: measure total variance and its spectrum separately, find a candidate bottleneck, then compare teacher, student, and equal-energy random directions while measuring quality as well as diversity. The transferable object is the controlled experiment, not the claim that all few-step generators share one universal collapse block.

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

- **Distillation:** Feature regression, LoRA, PCA steering, and early-step layout control all have predecessors. The contribution to isolate is block-local diagnosis plus teacher-directed repair. Include setup cost, FT-only and TF-only arms, random-direction controls, same-noise comparisons, and a quality-diversity frontier.
- **Generative geometry:** Compare clean/noise/velocity prediction separately from loss weighting. Match compute and guidance evaluations. Evaluate depth/poses using an independent source before promoting jointly generated disparity into a world-model claim.
- **RL/control:** Compare black-box RL against direct and short-horizon simulator-gradient learning when derivatives exist. Tune SAC, include real wall time and solver gradients, and test simulator mismatch before claiming a general advantage.
- **Neuro representations:** Match anatomy supervision and masking in baselines; report subject-level uncertainty, early-stage external validation, calibration, and stronger clinical endpoints. A supervised attention map is evidence of localization, not causal faithfulness.
- **Real-to-sim:** Separate compile success, render match, equilibrium, forced-rollout survival, and physical-parameter identification. Match repair budgets and validate observed interventions before claiming a real2sim2real loop.

## One-paragraph takeaway

The recurring design lesson is to test the interface a system would otherwise hide. A distilled generator can have plenty of variance in the wrong directions; a simple video model can benefit from making geometry share its pixel grid; a controller can learn faster when the simulator exposes derivatives; an anatomical token becomes meaningful only when losses constrain its content; and an attractive reconstructed scene may not survive gravity. None of these interfaces licenses a universal claim. Preserve the representation, its intervention test, and the failure boundary together.
