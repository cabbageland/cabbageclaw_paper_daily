## Ranked papers

### 1. **UGG: Unified Generative Geometry Model**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant generative modeling, 3D/4D representation, and geometric inference. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve. **Most relevant today.**
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.14440](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14440), November 10, 2026; arXiv lists NeurIPS 2026. [Project](https://ugg-model.github.io/) and [official code](https://github.com/amanikiruga/UGG). The public repository exposes inference/training code and documents downloadable weights; those weights were not downloaded or executed here.
- **Mechanism:** Treat RGB, depth, and camera rays as aligned modalities in one flow-matching model. Modality-specific branches exchange information through shared attention. Clean/noisy masks over modality and time specify the query; unavailable annotations are excluded from the loss. At test time, sample scene completions and rank geometry using RGB-derived correspondence evidence.
- **Why it matters:** Generation and perception become different conditionals of the same representation, rather than unrelated heads. Explicit depth and camera outputs also create a testable interface for rejecting plausible-looking geometry. This is a useful architectural baseline for joint generative/geometric modeling.
- **Evidence:** Sparse RealEstate10K translation angular error is 8.260 degrees for UGG, versus 39.834 for Aether, and 3.475 after 16-candidate search. Dense ScanNet++ ATE is 0.087 versus Geo4D's 0.190. In a separate smaller-model ablation, removing RGB generation raises KITTI depth Abs Rel from 0.152 to 0.322. Generation is competitive, not universally best: SEVA wins two-view DL3DV.
- **Catch:** Depth and pose are selected independently and may come from different candidates; the final pair is not guaranteed jointly consistent. Depth metrics use scale/shift alignment and dense pose uses Sim(3) alignment, not metric-scale recovery. Search relies on RAFT/RANSAC and proxy scores whose rules are selected leave-one-benchmark-out. Training uses 128 H200s for two weeks; 16 candidates are extra inference compute. This is not an action-conditioned physical simulator or a demonstrated planning system.
- **Read scope:** Full main text; dataset/modality handling, loss, training, sampling, alignment, and verifier-selection appendix passages; official repository README. No reproduction or code/proof audit. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/ugg_unified_generative_geometry.md).

### 2. **A Theory of Policy Optimization in Post-Training: Coverage Collapse and Reference Resets**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant RL/post-training and optimization design. **Verdict:** Highly relevant theoretical follow-up; lighter recommendation today.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.14467](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14467), November 10, 2026.
- **Mechanism:** Approximate Policy Mirror Descent repeatedly anchors a strongly KL-regularized inner problem to the latest policy, rather than permanently to the base model. Accurately solving those local problems preserves enough probability mass on optimal responses to keep learning tractable. The framework covers stylized PPO/GRPO and squared-loss Kimi/Rebel-style variants.
- **Why it matters:** A rising average reward can hide the loss of rare optimal responses. The reference policy is an optimization anchor, not merely an importance-sampling denominator. Resetting that anchor separates stable local learning from global progress.
- **Evidence:** An exact-gradient construction shows exponentially slow REINFORCE convergence despite favorable initial coverage, including with a fixed-reference KL penalty. The positive results give end-to-end sample/gradient complexity for softmax linear policies with reward linear in the same frozen features. Their approximate-update theorem controls coverage subject to explicit inner-solve tolerances; it is not just an empirical reward curve.
- **Catch:** This is a contextual-bandit/whole-response theory, not a proof for token-level transformer training or multi-step control. Practical PPO/GRPO are not identical to the analyzed variants. Strong regularization, adequate inner optimization, and realizability matter; changing the reference alone is not the theorem. No large-model experimental validation was established by this read.
- **Read scope:** Main problem definition, coverage-collapse argument, APMD theorem statements and intuition, algorithm instantiations, and limitations; appendices only partially acquired and proofs not independently checked. No detailed note today.

### 3. **Forward-Value Discretization: Faster Stochastic Diffusion Sampling Without High-Order Approximation**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant diffusion mechanisms and training-free inference. **Verdict:** Useful; implementation-worthy follow-up, lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.14328](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14328), November 10, 2026. The paper links [F-DDPM code](https://github.com/Na-Li66/F-DDPM); execution was not verified.
- **Mechanism:** Query the pretrained data/noise predictor at a lookahead state rather than automatically at the current state. F-DDPM accounts for the next noise injection using the previous prediction and previous noise. It needs one neural evaluation per iteration and remains first-order; better evaluation placement is the intervention, not a higher-order claim.
- **Why it matters:** Numerical convergence order and useful few-step generation quality are different axes. A simple state/query change can beat a higher-order approximation when the stochastic path error remains first-order anyway.
- **Evidence:** On unconditional CIFAR-10, F-DDPM with 15 evaluations reports FID 3.34, versus 7.07 for SDE-DPMSolver++2 at the same budget. The corresponding deterministic DPMSolver-3 uses only 10 evaluations and gets 3.88. Tests span CIFAR-10, ImageNet, FFHQ, and LSUN with 50K-sample FID. The theory gives matching first-order strong-error bounds for the analyzed stochastic solver families.
- **Catch:** Deterministic comparisons allocate stochastic methods 1.5 times the NFE, not equal compute. First-order lower bounds are for specified methods, coupling, intervals, and regularity assumptions—not an impossibility theorem for all stochastic samplers or a direct FID guarantee. Guidance behavior, inverse problems, and modern video/flow backbones remain open. Deterministic methods still win several settings.
- **Read scope:** Main sampler construction, convergence criteria/assumption descriptions, theorem scope, experimental protocols/tables, and discussion; partial appendices, no proof audit or implementation run. No detailed note today.

### 4. **OnlyAct: Extracting Action Representations from Foundation Models for World Models**

- **Classification:** Direct representation/world-model relevance across manipulation, navigation, games, and driving; not a closed-loop robot demonstration. **Verdict:** Useful; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.14420](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14420), November 10, 2026. [Project](https://runjiali-rl.github.io/onlyact/); the manuscript promises code/model release, which was not verified here.
- **Mechanism:** Adapt a V-JEPA 2 frame-pair encoder with LoRA and symmetric InfoNCE. Positives preserve motion while swapping scene content; same-video hard negatives discourage appearance shortcuts. Feed the resulting frozen action embeddings to an otherwise matched autoregressive video generator. Static-camera flow swapping becomes weaker photometric augmentation when the camera moves.
- **Why it matters:** Action information may already exist in a pretrained representation; the task is to remove nuisance without crushing expressiveness through a reconstruction bottleneck. Evaluate action probes, scene leakage, and transferred generation separately instead of treating next-frame fidelity as controllability.
- **Evidence:** On 12 continuous-action datasets, mean action-probe R-squared is 0.490 versus 0.347 for frozen V-JEPA 2 and 0.211 for the strongest retrained latent-action baseline. LIBERO transferred-flow direction cosine is 0.917 versus 0.803 for DreamDojo. Adaptation uses 168K unlabeled frame pairs; ten of thirteen probe datasets are unseen during adaptation, not necessarily unseen during foundation-model pretraining.
- **Catch:** Swapping scenes does not establish causal action identifiability; interactions and moving-camera scenes weaken the construction. Decoder-based scene leakage is a probe, not an invariance proof. Direct real-action control still uses labels and fine-tunes the action interface and denoiser. Reported evaluations are offline video/control proxies, not long-horizon physical execution or safe simulator validation.
- **Read scope:** Main encoder/objective/world-model methods and result tables, matched-backbone protocol, selected implementation/whitening passages, and limitations. No detailed note today.

### 5. **When Does the Real Wiring Win? Testing connectome structure-function claims against unmeasured physiology**

- **Classification:** Direct neuro/model-evaluation interest; adjacent architecture-ablation methodology. **Verdict:** Useful critical framing; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.14508](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14508), November 10, 2026. The submission provides ancillary code/data according to the paper; they were not executed here.
- **Mechanism:** Separate physiology tuned on real wiring and transferred to nulls (P1), physiology separately tuned for each wiring (P2), and tuning constrained by independent biological measurements (P3). Add placebo nulls, parameter sweeps, and a mirrored visual circuit with a constructed near-equivalent solution to expose protocol-induced structural advantages.
- **Why it matters:** A structured model beating a shuffled one can mean the baseline inherited the wrong parameters. Fair optimization and biologically plausible optimization answer different questions; a model that fails under measured physiology cannot adjudicate the animal's wiring.
- **Evidence:** In the mushroom-body model, the real-versus-cross-subtype-shuffle advantage reverses from about +0.09 under P1/P2 to -0.09 under P3. In flyvis, inherited parameters penalize a mirrored connectome more than a spatial shuffle in all 50 members, despite a constructed solution with median error gap about 0.07. Other claims, including dopamine-family segregation and topography for a persistent heading bump, survive the tested protocols.
- **Catch:** These are model/protocol findings, not a biological refutation of connectomes. P3 constrains one parameter in one circuit; only 11 held-out points support a key restricted estimate. The flyvis from-scratch pipeline does not reproduce original training, so converged null training remains unresolved. Parameter ranges, limited physiological flexibility, and approximate uncertainty intervals bound the conclusions.
- **Read scope:** Framework, theorem statements, three-circuit methods/results, physiological sensitivity, placebo analysis, discussion and limitations; selected supplementary passages, not a raw-data or proof audit. No detailed note today.

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

- **4D generation:** Compare any-to-any completion against fixed-direction models, and separate shared representation, additional supervision, backbone capacity, and search budget. Report unaligned geometry and jointly selected depth/pose where downstream use needs metric coherence.
- **RL:** Track optimal-response coverage or informative tail proxies alongside reward. Compare fixed and reset references with jointly tuned regularization, inner-solve budget, and sampling cost; do not cite the linear-policy theorem as a transformer guarantee.
- **Diffusion:** Match NFE and wall-clock separately. Evaluate query placement as well as order, and distinguish strong trajectory approximation from sample-distribution quality.
- **Action representations:** Include the frozen pretrained encoder, matched downstream generators, scene-leakage probes, and cross-scene action transfer. “Unlabeled adaptation” is not “label-free real control.”
- **Structured neuroscience/models:** Compare transferred parameters, independently optimized controls, and measurement-constrained controls. A symmetry-based placebo with a known solution is a stronger diagnostic than an arbitrarily destructive shuffle.

## One-paragraph takeaway

The best papers today specify what gets reused and what must remain free. UGG reuses a scene representation across conditional queries; reference resets reuse local policy progress without permanently anchoring to the base; forward-value sampling reuses previous estimates to improve where the model is queried. OnlyAct asks pretrained features to retain motion but shed scene identity, while the connectome paper shows how inherited physiology can rig a structural comparison. The transferable lesson is to audit the interface and the comparison—not just whether a model has an impressive prior.
