### 1. **Rethinking Contrastive Targets in Radiology Language-Image Pretraining**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant medical representation learning, compositional supervision, and explicit semantic structure. **Verdict:** Highly relevant; preserve. **Most relevant today.**
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.24016](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24016), Yunhe Gao and colleagues; NeurIPS 2026; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24016). The paper's [announced repository](https://github.com/yhygao/CorLIP) returned HTTP 404 during this run; code availability is not verified.
- **Mechanism:** CorLIP replaces CLIP's paired-identity target with a normalized compatibility matrix. Parse the paired report into a proxy image fact state and candidate sentences into assertions; an unpaired sentence is positive when its asserted facts hold for that state. Normalize rows and columns for symmetric contrastive cross-entropy. Sentences use hard entailment; bundled reports use partial agreement. The encoder architecture stays unchanged.
- **Why it matters:** An image can support many descriptions without being equivalent to their full reports. Treating those supported assertions as negatives can reward instance-specific shortcuts after semantic learning saturates. This is an actionable distinction between compatibility, global similarity, and label identity—not a new backbone wearing a clinical acronym.
- **Evidence:** Controlled CXR training shares DINOv3-initialized ViT-B/16, BioClinicalBERT, data, optimizer, and schedule. At 60k pairs, sentence CorLIP-CB gives peak/final MIMIC AUC 81.6/81.4 versus vanilla sentence 68.2/66.5. The less benchmark-coupled MedGemma variant reaches CheXpert/VinDr AUC 86.6/87.3 and MS-CXR pointing accuracy 41.6 versus vanilla sentence 82.1/83.1/30.7. Only corrected sentence configurations remain stable through batch 2048 among those tested. In the separate CT study, sentence correction improves peak AUC 75.8 to 79.3.
- **Catch:** The fact state comes from a report, not direct image adjudication. Schema granularity and extractor are confounded; CheXbert and the CT schema overlap their evaluation vocabularies. CXR uncertainty is mapped to absent. The paper reports no multi-seed significance analysis; in-domain peak checkpoints are selected retrospectively on test metrics for diagnosis, not deployment. Gradient decomposition is exact in logit space, but it is not a general theorem that a parameterized optimizer must collapse at large batch. Impression correction still degrades badly.
- **Read scope:** Full main text; extractor/compatibility Appendix C; split, model-selection, training, and CT Appendix E; selected theory and statistical-reporting checklist passages. PDF-based note, not an abstract-only summary; no reproduction. [Detailed note](../paper_notes/corlip_compatibility_targets.md).

### 2. **Counterfactual World Models: JEPAs That Reason About "What If"**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant causal world models and control; the ranking's one robotics-centered paper. **Verdict:** Highly relevant conditional mechanism; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.24778](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24778), Randall Balestriero, Yann LeCun, and Kerda Varaku; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24778), [announced code](https://github.com/galilai-group/counterfactual-WM).
- **Mechanism:** After observing a factual transition, predict the alternative outcome as observed outcome plus predicted alternative-minus-factual action contrast. Reuse the realized disturbance residual rather than predicting an average disturbance again. Short smooth transitions with separate action and disturbance channels make their interaction small; smoothness alone is insufficient. Correction can occur directly in features or after a physical-state readout.
- **Why it matters:** Factual likelihood does not identify which outcomes under different actions belong to the same hidden event. This gives a cheap, falsifiable counterfactual baseline while stating the extra structural assumption that makes it legitimate.
- **Evidence:** With unchanged factual models, four rendered simulators improve physical counterfactual R-squared from roughly 38–46% to 86–96% after a readout; feature-space correction, requiring no physical labels for the correction, reaches 73–94%. BlueROV2 needs substantially more data and benefits especially from the readout. Same-event hindsight success is 35–69% versus 14–23% for a simulator planner denied the observed outcome.
- **Catch:** That oracle comparison changes information access, not simulator accuracy. The highest scores use a state-supervised readout. Controlled simulations, supported actions, and unconfounded logged decisions do not establish real-world causal identification. Contacts, coordinate folds, and multiplicative actuator faults break the rule. Later actions benefit only when disturbances persist; without disturbances, residual correction reduces subsequent replanning success by 4–15 points. This is not long-horizon counterfactual rollout.
- **Read scope:** Introduction and identification framing; residual-reuse definition and exact error identity; short-time assumption summary; main empirical protocol, coordinate/history diagnostics, planning, and limitations. Long minimax and representation proofs were not exhaustively audited. No detailed note today.

### 3. **Elysium: Simulation-Free Unbalanced Schrödinger Bridges**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant generative stochastic dynamics and explicit population state; adjacent inspiration for scientific world models. **Verdict:** Useful, with unusually important identifiability limits; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.24763](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24763), Zihan Wang, Jianzhe Li, Zhenyi Zhang, and Peijie Zhou; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24763).
- **Mechanism:** Add a cemetery state to make changing-mass endpoint measures balanced, solve their entropic coupling, then sample analytic survival/exit bridge components. Regress drift with squared error and exit rate with Poisson relative entropy. Reverse declining intervals to handle growth, and learn entry locations with conditional flow matching. Training does not simulate trajectories; generation still does.
- **Why it matters:** A signed density-growth field does not specify individual entry and exit histories. Elysium makes that choice explicit and derives its training targets from the same reference process instead of stitching together unrelated objectives.
- **Evidence:** Five trained models per reported Elysium setting. Observed-population W1 on embryoid bodies is 7.748 versus WFR-FM's published 8.855; the more consequential omitted-time comparison is 9.770 versus 10.175. On synthetic killed-diffusion problems, independent reference solutions permit state and event-location checks, not just endpoint fit. Reported fits take 1.1–2.5 minutes including coupling and generation on the tested setup.
- **Catch:** Snapshot counts identify net change, not true cellular birth, death, or parentage. Each interval is either net gain or net loss; equal endpoint masses imply no events, so balanced turnover is invisible. Total-mass and event-time curves are fixed by endpoint masses and the chosen clock, not independently learned discoveries. Synthetic advantages partly reflect reference/objective matching; real-data WFR-FM/USB table entries are taken from their papers. State-dependent death rates generally remove the convenient closed forms.
- **Read scope:** Main reference, endpoint reduction, analytic components, regression objectives and recovery conditions; population matching, omitted-time evaluation, drug-response setup, and limitations. Appendix proofs and biological lineage validity were not independently established. No detailed note today.

### 4. **MINT: Meeting-time Indicators for Truncation in Multi-Step Off-Policy RL**

- **Classification:** Directly relevant non-agent RL, credit propagation, and estimator design. **Verdict:** Useful method with non-universal guarantees; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.24413](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24413), Seungyub Han, Taehyun Cho, Dohyeong Kim, Kyungjae Lee, and Jungwoo Lee; accepted at NeurIPS 2026 per arXiv; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24413), [announced code](https://github.com/seungyubhan/mint).
- **Mechanism:** Replace products of real-valued TD trace weights with a binary survival mask. Theory stops when coupled target and behavior state paths first meet. The practical surrogate keeps a replay step with probability 1 minus the clipped target/behavior action-probability ratio, using behavior log-probabilities stored at collection. Once stopped, the prefix stays stopped; a hard cap remains. Notably, identical policies yield one-step backups, not the longest trace.
- **Why it matters:** Backup length is part of the estimator, not merely a task-specific constant. Binary masks make squared weights equal survival indicators, but the sampled action rule and theoretical state coupling must remain separate objects.
- **Evidence:** Five-seed, five-task MuJoCo comparisons use a common cap of five. On eleven DMC tasks at 500k steps, IQM is 0.457 versus 0.366 for one-step SAC and 0.284 for ten-step SAC. In a three-seed RWRL diagnostic, cap 5 to 50 changes mean length only 3.9 to 4.8, with 99.5% stopping before cap 50.
- **Catch:** The variance result is at the true value function; separate sufficient fixed-policy contraction conditions do not certify deep actor–critic training. Atari mean improves but median and IQM are worse than published DrQ(epsilon). In learned-critic OGBench comparisons Retrace has lower RMSE on all evaluated datasets, despite favorable frozen-critic cells for MINT. Do not sell adaptive stopping as uniform variance or value-error dominance.
- **Read scope:** Main stopping definitions and implementation; variance/contraction scope; suite-specific protocols, aggregate results, shuffled-length control, and learned-critic counterevidence. The 81-page supplement was only selectively inspected. No detailed note today.

### 5. **Kernel Ridge Attention: Nonlinear Memory with Selective Forgetting**

- **Classification:** Adjacent inspiration for explicit memory and continual state updates; not a tool-agent paper. **Verdict:** Useful small-scale architecture lead; lighter recommendation.
- **Source:** [arXiv:2611.23896](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23896), Haowei Chen, Yichen Wang, and Congfu Xu; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23896).
- **Mechanism:** Each head stores regression coefficients and a full key-feature covariance. Learned Fourier features provide nonlinear associations; recursive ridge regression shapes writes using the inverse regularized key geometry. A content-dependent Ornstein–Uhlenbeck transition both decays coefficients and restores covariance toward a prior, reopening forgotten directions for new writes. Block scans and explicit adjoints parallelize the recurrence.
- **Why it matters:** Forgetting an old value without reopening its overconfident write direction can impede adaptation. The covariance is operational state, not decorative uncertainty terminology; its calibration as a real-world confidence estimate is nevertheless not established.
- **Evidence:** At about 31M parameters and 100M shared training tokens, perplexity is 88.00 versus Transformer++ 97.21 and Gated DeltaNet 110.95. Rare-repeat top-1 is 25.41% versus Gated DeltaNet 6.69%, but Transformer++ is better at 32.00%. State stays 1.165 MiB at 4k/8k prefixes; the optimized training step is 31.77 ms versus Gated DeltaNet 27.97 ms.
- **Catch:** One seed, one data order, 2k training windows, and a tiny language model. Controlled-copy accuracy is essentially chance, 50.39%, versus attention's 54.00%. Constant state does not imply constant recall quality over arbitrary context lengths. Full covariance costs quadratic feature-state storage and more compute than the tested delta-rule model; KDA/GDN-2 are recurrence controls, not full official systems. No large-model or long-horizon adaptation claim is earned.
- **Read scope:** Full main architecture, conditional kernel identity, parallelization, training/evaluation protocol, results, feature ablations, cost, and related work. Appendix numerical proofs and implementation were not reproduced. No detailed note today.

## Most relevant to cabbageland

**CorLIP.** Define the relation the model should learn before scaling the representation. Directional assertion compatibility preserves compositional semantics that instance identity and whole-report similarity obscure. The useful experiment is target replacement with matched encoders, plus a controlled synthetic reproduction of the failure—not the largest in-domain AUC.

## Novelty / framing / baseline impact

- **Contrastive structure:** Compare paired identity, report similarity, supervised multi-positive targets, and assertion compatibility under the same parser and training recipe. Test held-out findings, laterality, negation, uncertainty, and ontology granularity. Separate retrospective test peaks from validation-selected deployment results.
- **Counterfactual models:** Include residual reuse, factual-copy, outcome-blind simulator, and realized-disturbance oracle baselines. Separate feature-only from state-readout results and same-event replay from future replanning. Match information access before declaring that a learned system beats an oracle.
- **Generative dynamics:** Compare a solver against a reference-matched solver as well as different model families. Distinguish imposed mass clocks from learned spatial transport, and fitted snapshots from omitted-time predictions. Matching population distributions is not lineage recovery.
- **RL horizons:** Retain one-step, Retrace, fixed-horizon, fixed-lambda, and shuffled-length controls. Report learned-critic errors alongside frozen-critic diagnostics; keep state-coupling guarantees separate from action-surrogate guarantees.
- **Memory:** Compare full versus diagonal/isotropic covariance and decay-only versus uncertainty-restoring forgetting. Match nonlinear features and budgets. Report associative recall and latency, not perplexity and constant-state claims alone.

## One-paragraph takeaway

These papers become useful when their claims are narrowed to the relation actually modeled. A supported assertion need not identify its paired report; a shared hidden disturbance is not a fresh random future; inferred population events are reference-dependent rather than observed biology; a backup-survival rule is not a universal critic improvement; a covariance-shaped memory write is not unlimited recall. The steal-worthy move is to expose that relation as state, supervision, or a testable assumption—and then keep the failure cases in the headline reasoning.
